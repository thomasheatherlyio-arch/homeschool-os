## ☀️ Today
1. **Open COLT** (the COLT icon on the desktop) and say **"COLT, morning"**. It tells you your first move.
2. **This week's missions:** [[NOW]] (the tab next to this one). Do one, then tick its box.
3. **Today's note:** click the calendar icon on the left (or Ctrl+P → "Open today's daily note").
   Write 1–3 lines: what you did, what's next.
4. **Finished something?** Tell COLT `I finished <thing>`. Or make a note in `50-evidence/` and
   Ctrl+P → "Insert template" → **evidence**. Then show Dad the proof. It counts once he checks it.
5. **Done for the day:** "COLT, wrap up", then close COLT. Fridays: "weekly review".

> Stuck, a red Git error, or COLT wants to run a command? Stop and get Dad.
