## 2. Quadrant Map

| Component | Primary Quadrant ([[01-holistic-quadrants-the-circle|Doc 01]]) | Secondary |
|---|---|---|
| Why electricians, right now (Week 1 / Section 8) | 🧠 Mind & Mastery | 🚀 Venture & Craft |
| Electrical safety & tools | 🗂️ Systems & Self-Command | 💪 Body & Discipline |
| Engineering fundamentals (physics/math) | 🧠 Mind & Mastery | — |
| Math of the trade (wire sizing, load calcs) | 🧠 Mind & Mastery | 🗂️ Systems & Self-Command |

Like The Kitchen, this course is anchored in **Venture & Craft** — something real gets built and a real business plan gets pitched — but it pulls even harder into **Mind & Mastery** than [[kitchen-course|Doc 14]] did, on purpose, since engineering fundamentals are half the point.
