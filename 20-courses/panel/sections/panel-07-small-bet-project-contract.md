## 7. Small Bet & Project Contract

Per [[07-north-star-life-path|Doc 07]] §3, this starts as a small bet, not a locked-in 3-month commitment — and per [[04-student-dashboard|Doc 04]] §3, a project this size gets its own lightweight contract, modeled on [[00-governing-charter-and-toc|Doc 00]] §7.

**Suggested first small bet:** *"Run Weeks 1–4 (orientation, safety, and the two engineering-fundamentals weeks) over the next 4–5 weeks. At the end, log it in the Interest Ledger honestly: does the math/physics side of this actually hold his attention, or was the AI-demand story more interesting than the trade itself? Either answer is useful data."*

A project contract should cover:

- **What Shiloh commits to:** showing up to scheduled sessions, finishing each week's deliverable rather than leaving problem sets half-done, and treating the safety rules in Section 5 as non-negotiable.
- **What Thomas provides:** any trainer-kit/multimeter materials needed, direct supervision for all hands-on and panel-walkthrough sessions, and time in the weekly plan.
- **What "done" looks like per week:** the deliverable in the Section 4 table, actually finished and logged.
- **Review point:** end of Week 4 (the small-bet checkpoint above), and again at each Friday/Quarterly review after that.
