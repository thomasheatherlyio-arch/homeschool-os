## 12. Career & Interest-Exploration Tie-In

Per Doc 10, this only becomes a real Interest Ledger candidate once it's been tested, not just enjoyed in the abstract:

- **Rung 1 (informational interview):** Week 11 is built for exactly this — a working electrician, ideally someone who's done data-center or commercial work, asked Doc 10 §4's standard five questions (how'd you get in, what's a normal week, what's the pay range, what's the worst part nobody tells you, who would you introduce me to next).
- **Rung 2 (job shadow):** the natural next step if the Week 11 contact is open to it — same Headmaster sign-off process as any other outside placement ([[00-governing-charter-and-toc|Doc 00]] §4, Doc 10 §4).
- **Rung 5 (dual-credit CTE):** per Doc 09 §6 Option A and Section 9 above, an electrical Career Academy track at a community college is a real, checkable near-term target once Shiloh hits the relevant STAAR/ACT/SAT readiness benchmarks — worth flagging on [[04-student-dashboard|Doc 04]]'s tracking if this interest survives the small bet.
- **Rung 6 (registered apprenticeship):** the real entry point at age 16, via a program like the South Texas Electrical JATC (Doc 10 §3).

Log any of this in the Exposure Log (Doc 10 §6) and Interest Ledger ([[07-north-star-life-path|Doc 07]] §6) as it happens.
