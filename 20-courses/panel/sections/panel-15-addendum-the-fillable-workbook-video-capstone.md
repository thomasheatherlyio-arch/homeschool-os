## 14a. Addendum — The Fillable Workbook & Video Capstone

*Added 2026-08-24, per Thomas.* This course's 12 weeks got turned into a real, daily-fillable syllabus rather than staying a reference doc: a Word document (built to open cleanly in Google Docs, same convention as [[kitchen-course|Doc 14]]'s Opening Night workbook) that Shiloh fills in every day he works on the course. It lives outside the project as a delivered file, not as a project doc, so future sessions should know it exists even though its content isn't stored here.

**Structure:** front matter (how-to-use instructions, a materials checklist, a video-production/editing primer, the course-at-a-glance syllabus table, a running Video Log/Shot List, and the Section 7 small-bet contract, fillable) followed by all 12 weekly sections, each with: teaching notes condensed from Sections 3–12 above, that week's worksheet deliverable, a Daily Log table (Monday–Friday rows, filled in every day per [[04-student-dashboard|Doc 04]]'s rhythm), and a Friday Review block with the five-quadrant score table and a Headmaster sign-off line.

**The video capstone thread:** every week carries one short 🎥 video-clip assignment (60 seconds to 3 minutes, prompts included) that Shiloh films and logs in the Video Log as he goes — "Meet the Trade" in Week 1 through "The Interview" in Week 11. Week 12 pairs the Section 11 build-and-pitch capstone with a full step-by-step guide to compiling all 12 clips into one final documentary-style video (gather → script the connective narration → rough cut → polish with titles/music → export and review with Thomas), making basic video production and editing a real, graded strand of this course rather than an optional add-on — the same multidisciplinary logic [[11-the-studio-experiential-learning-menu|Doc 11]]'s Studio pillars already use elsewhere in this system.

**Closes the loop back to [[07-north-star-life-path|Doc 07]]:** the workbook's final pages are a Course Reflection (Interest Ledger–style: confirmed / ruled out / still unclear) and a blank Evidence Inbox log for transfer into [[03-academic-tracker-tx-compliance|Doc 03]], so the course resolves into the rest of the system rather than sitting apart from it.
