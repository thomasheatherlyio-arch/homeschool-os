## 3. Texas Curriculum Tie-In (per [[03-academic-tracker-tx-compliance|Doc 03]])

| Subject | TX status ([[03-academic-tracker-tx-compliance|Doc 03]] §1) | How The Panel covers it | Evidence type |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mathematics** | Required | Ohm's/Watt's Law algebra, unit conversion (volts/amps/watts/kWh), wire-gauge ampacity tables, voltage-drop calculations, conduit-fill geometry, business math (bidding/estimating, Section 10) | Worked problem sets, load-calc worksheets, a costed-out bid sheet |
| **Reading** | Required | Reading the NEC (National Electrical Code), technical spec sheets, blueprints/one-line diagrams, licensing statutes (TDLR) | Reading log, annotated code excerpts, source notes |
| **Spelling & Grammar** | Required | Written safety checklist, informational-interview write-up, the Section 11 business-plan pitch document | Drafts, finished write-ups |
| **Good Citizenship** | Required | Why licensing exists (public safety, not gatekeeping), the electrical inspector's role in local government, how code adoption works in Texas | Research report, reflection |
| **Science** | Standard practice | Physics of electricity (voltage/current/resistance, series vs. parallel circuits, AC vs. DC, basic electromagnetism), engineering safety-margin concepts | Circuit-build experiment write-ups, a self-built safety checklist |
| **History / Social Studies** | Standard practice | Short history-of-electrification unit (Edison/Westinghouse "War of Currents," rural electrification, the modern grid) | Short research report or presentation |
| **Health & PE** | Standard practice | Jobsite physical safety — ladder safety, PPE, ergonomics of trade work, lockout/tagout discipline | Safety checklist, Thomas sign-off |
| **Executive Function** | Core to the plan ([[03-academic-tracker-tx-compliance|Doc 03]] §4) | Planning a multi-week hands-on project, running the informational interview outreach, building a real project timeline for the capstone pitch | Weekly mission logs, Friday review notes |

Log every right-hand-column item into [[03-academic-tracker-tx-compliance|Doc 03]]'s Evidence Log as it's produced — same discipline as The Kitchen and The Gym.
