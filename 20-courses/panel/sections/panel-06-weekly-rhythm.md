## 6. Weekly Rhythm

This plugs into the normal [[04-student-dashboard|Doc 04]] rhythm rather than replacing it:

- **Monday planning:** one of the week's 3–5 missions can be a Panel unit step — e.g., "solve this week's Ohm's Law problem set" or "draft the informational-interview outreach message."
- **Sessions:** actual course time — math work, reading, hands-on builds, research. Capture evidence same-day per [[04-student-dashboard|Doc 04]]'s "capture as you go" habit (a photo of the built circuit, the worked problem set, the research notes).
- **Friday review:** log finished pieces into the Evidence Inbox, tag them to the right quadrant (Section 2) and subject (Section 3), and check whether the pace still fits — same check-in point The Kitchen and The Gym use.
