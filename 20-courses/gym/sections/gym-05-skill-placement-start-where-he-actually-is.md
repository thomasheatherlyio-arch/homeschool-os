## 5. Skill Placement — Start Where He Actually Is

Run a quick, honest self-check with Shiloh before Unit 1 locks in (COLT can run this conversationally), same approach as [[kitchen-course|Doc 14]] §5:

- **Already training regularly with Jahari, comfortable with basic lifts/bodyweight movements** — the likely starting point, given the basketball work already underway. Unit 1 can compress to a form/safety review rather than starting from zero; move faster into Units 2–4.
- **Solid on the "doing" but has never studied the "why"** — Units 2–3 (physiology, training types) are where the real new ground is; this is likely the highest-value stretch of the course for Shiloh specifically.
- **New to structured tracking and program design** — Units 4–5 deserve the full pace; this is genuinely new executive-function territory, not a review.

Any strength/conditioning work in this course under Thomas's supervision is a home-training context, same as [[kitchen-course|Doc 14]] §5's home-kitchen framing — not a substitute for Jahari's direct basketball coaching, and not paid work. If personal training ever becomes a real Rung 4 job candidate (Section 8), that's a later, separate decision.
