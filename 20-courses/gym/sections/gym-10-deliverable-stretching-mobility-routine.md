## 10. Deliverable — Stretching & Mobility Routine

Built for a 14-year-old athlete, with the flat-feet corrective work (Section 9) folded directly into the warm-up rather than bolted on as an extra step. Run the warm-up before any training session (basketball, lifting, or conditioning); run the cool-down after.

### 10.1 Dynamic Warm-Up (~8–10 minutes, before training)

| # | Move | Dose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | March in place / high knees | 30 seconds |
| 2 | Leg swings (front-to-back, then side-to-side) | 10 each direction, each leg |
| 3 | Walking lunge with torso twist | 8–10 each leg |
| 4 | Inchworms (walk hands out to a plank, walk feet up) | 5 reps |
| 5 | **Ankle circles** *(flat-feet mobility)* | 10 each direction, each foot |
| 6 | **Short-foot arch doming** *(flat-feet activation, Section 9.3 Phase 1)* | 10 reps, 5-sec hold, each foot |
| 7 | **Toe yoga — big toe lifts** *(flat-feet activation)* | 10 each foot |
| 8 | Bodyweight squats | 10 reps |
| 9 | Arm circles / shoulder rolls | 10 each direction |
| 10 | High knees or butt kicks (moving) | 20 yards each |

### 10.2 Static Cool-Down (~8–10 minutes, after training)

Hold each stretch 20–30 seconds per side, breathing normally, no bouncing.

| # | Stretch | Targets |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Standing quad stretch | Front of thigh |
| 2 | Standing hamstring stretch / forward fold | Back of thigh, low back |
| 3 | **Calf stretch against a wall** *(counters the tight-calf driver of flat feet)* | Gastrocnemius |
| 4 | **Bent-knee (soleus) calf stretch** | Deep calf |
| 5 | Kneeling hip flexor stretch | Front of hip |
| 6 | Figure-4 glute/piriformis stretch | Glutes/hip rotators |
| 7 | Seated butterfly stretch | Groin/inner thigh |
| 8 | Cross-body shoulder stretch | Shoulders |
| 9 | **Plantar fascia stretch (toes against wall or ball roll)** *(flat-feet specific)* | Arch/plantar fascia |
| 10 | Child's pose | Low back, hips |

### 10.3 Standalone Flat-Feet Session (3×/week, ~10 minutes, separate from training days)

On non-training days, or added onto the warm-up 3 nights a week, run the fuller Section 9.3 corrective block (short foot holds, towel scrunches, single-leg calf raises, heel walking, single-leg balance) as its own mini-session — this is the piece that actually builds new strength over the 3–6 month window, rather than just maintaining mobility.
