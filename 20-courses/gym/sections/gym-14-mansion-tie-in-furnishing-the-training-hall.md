## 14. Mansion Tie-In — Furnishing the Training Hall

[[08-mind-mansion-living-library|Doc 08]] §2's 💪 Training Hall room has sat empty since the Mansion was built. This course is the natural first furniture for it — candidates for the Study Shelves ([[08-mind-mansion-living-library|Doc 08]] §3) and Practice Room ([[08-mind-mansion-living-library|Doc 08]] §6), pending Shiloh's actual pick:

- **The Study Shelves (Training Hall):** an accessible strength-training or exercise-science book (candidates to bring to Shiloh rather than pre-assign, per [[08-mind-mansion-living-library|Doc 08]] §9's protocol), and — once certification study actually starts — the official NASM or ACE study material itself.
- **The Practice Room:** the Section 10 stretching/mobility routine and the Section 9.3 flat-feet corrective work are exactly the kind of recurring habit this room is built to track (frequency, status, notes) rather than a one-time consumption item.
