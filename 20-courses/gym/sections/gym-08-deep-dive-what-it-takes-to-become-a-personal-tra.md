## 8. Deep Dive — What It Takes to Become a Personal Trainer

*Researched August 2026. Requirements and pricing shift — reverify directly with the certifying body before Shiloh actually enrolls, especially once he's closer to 18.*

### 8.1 The Core Requirements

Every major certifying body (NASM, ACE, ISSA, ACSM, NSCA/CSCS) requires:

- **Minimum age 18** and a high school diploma or equivalent (GED-eligible per Doc 09).
- **Current CPR/AED certification**, required before sitting the exam — this has no meaningful age barrier and Shiloh could realistically earn this well before 18, as a real stretch-goal credential in its own right.
- **Passing a proctored certification exam.** Using NASM as the reference example: 120 questions, a 2-hour limit, and a scaled score of 70 to pass (NASM reports an 85% pass rate in 2025).

### 8.2 The Certification Landscape

| Certification | Approx. 2026 Cost | Accreditation Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **NASM-CPT** | $999–$1,399 | NCCA-accredited; widely recognized by gyms/employers |
| **ACE-CPT** | $989–$1,750 | NCCA-accredited; widely recognized |
| **ISSA-CPT** | ~$1,068 | NCCA-accredited exam available through a separate pathway (NCCPT) — worth confirming which package includes it |
| **ACSM-CPT** | $939–$994 | NCCA-accredited, research/clinical-leaning |
| **NSCA-CSCS** | $500–$1,000+ | NCCA-accredited; the standard for strength & conditioning coaching specifically (often paired with a bachelor's degree requirement) |

NASM and ACE are the two most consistently cited as the "gold standard" pairing for a gym-floor personal training career; CSCS is the discipline-specific choice if Shiloh's interest ends up leaning toward athletic performance/strength coaching rather than general personal training — worth revisiting once basketball-specific coaching interest (if any) is clearer.

### 8.3 What This Means for Shiloh, Specifically, Right Now

At 14, actual certification is roughly **4 years out** — which makes this a long-horizon Interest Ledger candidate ([[07-north-star-life-path|Doc 07]] §3), not a near-term milestone. That doesn't make the next 4 years dead time:

- **Study the content now.** The course material behind these certifications (Units 1–7 of this doc) is learnable well before the exam is legally sittable — Shiloh can genuinely know the material years ahead of being allowed to test on it.
- **Earn CPR/AED certification now** as a real, standalone credential.
- **Build a real portfolio now** — a working training log, a real designed program (Section 11), and real experience actually training someone (a family member, to start) are exactly the kind of evidence a certifying body or future employer would want to see anyway, and they're achievable today.
- **Treat this like Doc 10's exposure ladder.** An informational interview with a working personal trainer (Rung 1) or a gym shadow day (Rung 2) is available now, the same as any trade on Doc 10's menu — worth adding "personal trainer / strength coach" as a formal Interest Ledger and Exposure Log candidate if this course sticks.
