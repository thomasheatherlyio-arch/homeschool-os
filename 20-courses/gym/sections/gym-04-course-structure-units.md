## 4. Course Structure — Units

Suggested pace: roughly **1–2 weeks per unit**, running inside existing Body & Discipline training time (this replaces or upgrades some of that time, not stacks on top of it). Units 1–2 and the flat-feet self-assessment in Unit 7 should start early and then **run continuously in the background** for the rest of the course — a stretching/mobility routine and a training log aren't things you finish, they're things you keep doing.

| Unit | Focus | Key skills | Deliverable / evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Foundations & Safety** | Basic anatomy, warm-up/cool-down structure, proper form, injury prevention | Major muscle groups, joint-friendly form cues, why warm-ups exist | A self-built training-safety checklist; the stretching routine in Section 10, actually run |
| **2. Exercise Physiology & Energy Systems** | What's happening inside the body during exercise | Aerobic vs. anaerobic, the three energy systems, muscle fiber types | A one-page "how my body makes energy" summary (Section 6) |
| **3. Types of Fitness — The Training Disciplines** | The major training methodologies and what each is actually optimizing for | Powerlifting, calisthenics, bodybuilding, endurance/aerobic training, functional/mixed training | A comparison chart of 4+ disciplines: goal, method, energy system emphasis (Section 6) |
| **4. Program Design & Progressive Overload** | How a real program is built and progressed over time | Sets/reps/volume, percentage-of-max, progressive overload methods | A written 1-week sample program with a stated progression rule |
| **5. Workout Tracking Systems** | Making progress measurable instead of felt | Volume load, RPE, log formats (paper vs. app) | A working training log template, used for at least 2 real weeks (Section 7) |
| **6. Nutrition Fundamentals** | What fuels training and recovery | Macronutrients, calorie needs, hydration, meal timing basics | A one-day nutrition breakdown of Shiloh's own eating, compared to active-teen guidelines (Section 8) |
| **7. Assessment & Corrective Exercise** | Reading a body and correcting real issues | Basic movement/posture assessment; Shiloh's own flat-feet case study | The flat-feet research summary + corrective protocol, actually started (Section 9) |
| **8. The Business & Profession of Personal Training** | What it actually takes to do this for real | Certification landscape, scope of practice, client relations basics | The certification research summary (Section 8) |
| **Capstone — Design & Run a Real Program** | Everything above, applied to an actual person | Full program design, delivery, and coaching a real session | See Section 12 |
