## 17. Weekly Rhythm

Plugs into the normal [[04-student-dashboard|Doc 04]] rhythm, same as [[kitchen-course|Doc 14]] §6:

- **Monday planning:** one of the week's missions can be a Gym unit step — e.g., "research and compare two more training disciplines" or "log 3 sessions in the tracking template."
- **Daily:** the Section 10 warm-up/cool-down runs every training day, no exceptions — capture evidence same-day (log entry, a photo of a written program, a note).
- **Friday review:** log finished pieces into the Evidence Inbox, tag to quadrant (Section 2) and subject (Section 3), and check whether the flat-feet protocol (Section 9) is actually happening 3×/week.
