## 3. Texas Curriculum Tie-In (per [[03-academic-tracker-tx-compliance|Doc 03]])

| Subject | TX status ([[03-academic-tracker-tx-compliance|Doc 03]] §1) | How The Gym covers it | Evidence type |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Health & Physical Education** | Standard practice | The entire course — training practice, nutrition science, program design | Training log, program documents, session notes |
| **Science** | Standard practice | Exercise physiology (energy systems, muscle fiber types, cardiovascular adaptation), anatomy/kinesiology basics, the flat-feet biomechanics case study | Unit write-ups, the flat-feet research summary (Section 9) |
| **Mathematics** | Required | Volume-load math (weight × reps × sets), percentage-of-max calculations, calorie/macro math, progress-tracking data over time | Tracking log, program design worksheets |
| **Reading** | Required | Reading certification/study material, research on training methods and nutrition, source evaluation | Reading/research notes, source list |
| **Spelling & Grammar** | Required | Writing an actual client-facing program (Section 11), a training log, written reflections | Draft programs, written client notes |
| **Good Citizenship** | Required | Coaching/training a real person (a family member) safely and responsibly; understanding a trainer's real duty of care and scope of practice (Section 8) | Client session notes, safety checklist |
| **Executive Function** | Core to the plan ([[03-academic-tracker-tx-compliance|Doc 03]] §4) | Running a consistent training log across weeks, planning and adjusting a real program on schedule | Weekly mission logs, Friday review notes |

Every deliverable in the right-hand column should get an entry in [[03-academic-tracker-tx-compliance|Doc 03]]'s evidence log, same as [[kitchen-course|Doc 14]].
