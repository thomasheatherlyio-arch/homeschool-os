## 22. The Companion Workbook — Fillable Syllabus & Video Log

*Added 2026-08-24, per Thomas.* Section 21's six-week structure now exists as an actual fillable document — **"The Gym — Client Zero to Coach"** — built to open cleanly in Google Docs (upload the `.docx` to Drive and it converts automatically) or stay in Word, the same delivery pattern as [[kitchen-course|Doc 14]] §13a's *Opening Night* workbook. It lives outside the project as a file delivered directly to Thomas, so this section is the pointer future sessions need.

**What's in it (21 pages):** a cover/info page; a one-page at-a-glance syllabus table; a **Video Production Primer** — shooting basics, a beginner-friendly editing-app comparison (CapCut, iMovie, Clipchamp), and the core editing skills checklist, taught up front since filming starts in Week 1; then all six weeks from Section 21, each broken into its two sessions with date/time lines, guided fill-in questions and tables (energy systems, the discipline comparison chart, the sample program, the tracking log, the macronutrient and nutrition-breakdown tables, the full five-question fasting research summary with its Option A/B decision box, the flat-feet baseline-vs-progress table, the certification worksheet, and the Week 6 client intake/program/session log), a reflection prompt and Headmaster check-in line every session, and a 🎥 video-clip prompt box slotted exactly where each of the twelve clips from Section 21 gets recorded.

**The multidisciplinary piece Thomas asked for:** the workbook closes with a dedicated **Final Capstone Video Project** section — gathering all twelve clips, storyboarding their order, an editing checklist (trim, arrange, title cards, balance audio, export), and instructions for filing the finished video in the Evidence Inbox ([[04-student-dashboard|Doc 04]] §2.3) and The Gallery ([[08-mind-mansion-living-library|Doc 08]] §8). This makes video production and editing a real, graded strand of the course alongside the fitness science, not an optional add-on — Shiloh finishes with a compiled video of his own six weeks, not just twelve loose clips.

**Sign-off page:** mirrors [[00-governing-charter-and-toc|Doc 00]] §7's contract format — Student-CEO and Headmaster signatures, plus a Headmaster's-notes block.
