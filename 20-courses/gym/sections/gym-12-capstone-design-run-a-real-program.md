## 12. Capstone — Design & Run a Real Program

**Required minimum:** Shiloh assesses a real person (Section 7's Unit tie-in — himself counts, but a family member is stronger evidence of the actual skill of *training someone else*), designs a program in the Section 11 format matched to that person's real starting point and goals, runs at least one full session coaching them through it live — including leading the warm-up, coaching form, and logging the session — and writes a short reflection on what worked and what he'd change.

**Stretch goal (optional):** run the program with that client for a full 2–4 week cycle, applying and logging real progressive overload, and present a short "before vs. after" summary (numbers from the log, not a vibe) — this is the [[07-north-star-life-path|Doc 07]] §3 small-bet-sized version of the bigger swing Section 8.3 describes.
