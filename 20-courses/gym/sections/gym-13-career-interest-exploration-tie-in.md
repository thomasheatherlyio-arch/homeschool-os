## 13. Career & Interest-Exploration Tie-In

Per Doc 10, personal training only becomes a real Interest Ledger candidate once it's been tested, not just enjoyed in the abstract:

- **Rung 1 (informational interview):** a working personal trainer or strength coach — Jahari may know one, or this is a natural cold-outreach candidate using Doc 10 §4's script, adapted ("How did you get certified, what does a normal week of clients actually look like, what's the part nobody tells you about?").
- **Rung 2 (job shadow):** a gym-floor shadow day with a working trainer, once a contact exists — same Headmaster sign-off process as any other outside placement ([[00-governing-charter-and-toc|Doc 00]] §4, Doc 10 §4).
- **[[13-the-work-ladder-home-job-and-first-job|Doc 13]] tie-in:** training a family member for real (starting with the Capstone in Section 12) can become a paid "personal training" line on the Work Ladder's Extras Menu ([[13-the-work-ladder-home-job-and-first-job|Doc 13]] §2.2), the same bridge [[kitchen-course|Doc 14]] §10 describes for family-chef work — real, valued household contribution now, ahead of any formal certification later.
- **Long-horizon note:** actual certification (Section 8) sits behind the age-18 door, which makes this a multi-year Interest Ledger thread rather than a near-term milestone — worth revisiting explicitly at each Quarterly Headmaster Review to see if it's still pulling Shiloh's interest.

Log any of this in the Exposure Log (Doc 10 §6) and Interest Ledger ([[07-north-star-life-path|Doc 07]] §6) as it happens.
