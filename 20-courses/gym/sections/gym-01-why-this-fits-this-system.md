## 1. Why This Fits This System

Same logic [[kitchen-course|Doc 14]] §1 laid out for cooking, applied to training:

- **Every workout is a physiology experiment** — energy systems, muscle fiber recruitment, and adaptation are happening whether or not anyone names them (Unit 2), which is a natural home for [[11-the-studio-experiential-learning-menu|Doc 11]] §2.5-style edutainment ("why does lifting heavy things make you stronger?").
- **Every program is a math problem** — sets × reps × load = volume, percentages of a max lift, calorie and macro math, progress-over-time tracking (Units 4–5).
- **A training log is executive-function practice in disguise** — showing up on a schedule, logging honestly, adjusting the plan when it isn't working is [[01-holistic-quadrants-the-circle|Doc 01]]'s Systems & Self-Command quadrant, wearing a lifting belt.
- **A body actually changes** — per [[00-governing-charter-and-toc|Doc 00]] Principle 3, nobody has to take Shiloh's word for it that training is working; a logged program, a before/after strength number, and a body that moves better are the paper trail.
- **It's a real career candidate, not just a hobby.** Personal training is a licensable, chartable profession with a real certification path (Section 8) — which makes it a legitimate Doc 10 Interest Ledger candidate the same way HVAC or welding is, and a legitimate future line on [[13-the-work-ladder-home-job-and-first-job|Doc 13]]'s Work Ladder Extras Menu once Shiloh has real skill to offer (training a family member, eventually a paid client).
- **It gives Shiloh something the Mansion doesn't have yet.** [[08-mind-mansion-living-library|Doc 08]] §2's Training Hall room has sat empty since the Mansion was built — this course is what furnishes it.
