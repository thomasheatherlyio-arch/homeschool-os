## 21. Addendum — The 6-Week Structured Course: "Client Zero to Coach"

*Added 2026-08-24, per Thomas.* Mirroring [[kitchen-course|Doc 14]] §13a's "Opening Night" treatment, this addendum turns the Section 16 small bet into a concrete, scoped 6-week run: Shiloh spends the first five weeks becoming a genuinely well-trained client of his own program — physiology, tracking, nutrition, and his own flat-feet correction — then spends the sixth week turning around and coaching someone else through it, the actual test of whether "personal trainer" survives contact with reality as an Interest Ledger candidate (Section 13).

**Structure:** 6 weeks, 2 work sessions/week (12 sessions total), one written deliverable due at the end of most weeks, capped by a real Capstone coaching session in Week 6.

| Week | Focus | Ties to Sections Above |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Foundations, Safety & Baseline — build the safety checklist, start the Section 10 warm-up/cool-down daily, run a flat-feet baseline self-assessment | Unit 1 (§4), §9, §10 |
| 2 | How the Body Makes Energy — energy systems, muscle fiber types, aerobic vs. anaerobic, build the training-discipline comparison chart | Units 2–3 (§4), §6 |
| 3 | Building a Real Program — sets/reps/volume, progressive overload, stand up the tracking log and start logging real sessions | Units 4–5 (§4), §7 |
| 4 | Fueling the Body — nutrition fundamentals + the fasting research deep-dive and (if approved) the Option A observation log | Unit 6 (§4), §8 (nutrition), §20 |
| 5 | Correct & Study the Profession — deepen flat-feet corrective work against the Week 1 baseline; research the certification landscape and draft an informational-interview outreach message | Unit 7–8 (§4), §9.3, §8 (certification), §13 |
| 6 | Coach Someone For Real — design a full program for an actual family member per Section 11's format, then run the session live: lead the warm-up, coach form, log it, and write a closing reflection | Capstone (§12) |

**The deliverables, week by week:**
- **End of Week 1:** the training-safety checklist, and a logged flat-feet baseline (photos of standing arches, ankle dorsiflexion check, one-line notes) to measure real progress against by Week 5.
- **End of Week 2:** the "how my body makes energy" one-pager, and the training-discipline comparison chart (§6.4).
- **End of Week 4:** the written fasting research summary (§20.7), plus, only if Thomas has signed off, the Option A observation log (§20.6).
- **End of Week 5:** a flat-feet progress note against the Week 1 baseline, the certification research summary in Shiloh's own words, and a drafted (not necessarily sent) informational-interview outreach message.
- **End of Week 6 (Capstone):** the real client's program document, the coached session actually run and logged, a written reflection, and an honest Interest Ledger entry — confirmed / ruled out / still unclear — closing the loop the same way [[kitchen-course|Doc 14]] §13a's workbook does.

**Optional Studio tie-in (per Section 15):** a short recorded explainer at the end of Week 2 ("how does my body actually make energy for a workout?" — [[11-the-studio-experiential-learning-menu|Doc 11]] §2.5 Edutainment format) and, if Shiloh wants it, the Week 6 capstone session filmed as Learning Log or Day in the Life content. Both stay private/family-only by the [[11-the-studio-experiential-learning-menu|Doc 11]] §6 default unless Thomas signs off on anything further.

**Optional stretch checkbox:** starting CPR/AED certification (Section 8.1) during Week 5 — real, achievable now, and a genuine credential regardless of how the rest of the course lands.

**Closes the loop back to [[07-north-star-life-path|Doc 07]]:** the Week 6 Interest Ledger entry is the point of the whole six weeks — this small bet is meant to actually resolve one way or another, not just fade into "still working out sometimes."
