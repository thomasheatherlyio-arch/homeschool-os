# Doc 15 — The Gym: Personal Training & Fitness Science Course

**Purpose:** Shiloh has chosen physical fitness as a new activity-based elective — not just working out, but learning how *training itself* actually works, the way [[kitchen-course|Doc 14]] turned cooking into a real course instead of just making dinner. This doc builds that: a structured, self-paced curriculum that treats becoming a personal trainer as a legitimate career/life-skill track (per Doc 10's exposure-ladder logic and [[13-the-work-ladder-home-job-and-first-job|Doc 13]]'s Work Ladder) *and* a vehicle for real Texas-curriculum subject credit (per [[03-academic-tracker-tx-compliance|Doc 03]]) — Health & PE most directly, but also Science, Math, Reading, and Good Citizenship, taught through a training log and a barbell instead of a workbook.

This course runs alongside, not instead of, Shiloh's existing basketball work with Jahari ([[02-knights-of-the-round-table|Doc 02]]) — Jahari coaches the sport and the mental-toughness/zen side; this course teaches the *underlying science of training itself* — physiology, program design, nutrition, and tracking — so Shiloh understands *why* his own training works, and can eventually apply that skill to other people, not just himself.
