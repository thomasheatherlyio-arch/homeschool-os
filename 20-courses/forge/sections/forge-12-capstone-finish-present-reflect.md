## 12. Capstone — Finish, Present, Reflect

**Required minimum:** Shiloh finishes the Week 9 rough-cut short film to a genuinely completed state — edited, paced, with titles/credits — and presents it to Thomas, walking through the actual production pipeline he used (script/idea → AI-generated visuals → voice/audio → edit) and naming, honestly, which parts AI actually helped with and which parts still needed his own judgment and work.

**Alternate capstone (Shiloh's choice, in place of the film):** if the technical/building side of the course (Weeks 2–3) pulled harder than the creative-production side, Shiloh can instead extend and polish his Week 3 trained-model project into something more ambitious — a more accurate classifier, a more complete Scratch game built around it — and present that instead, with the same "walk through the pipeline and be honest about what worked" standard.

**Either version:**
- Gets logged in the Evidence Inbox ([[04-student-dashboard|Doc 04]] §2.3) with the finished piece and a short written reflection.
- Closes with an honest Interest Ledger entry ([[07-north-star-life-path|Doc 07]] §6) — confirmed / ruled out / still unclear — on whether AI (technical side, creative side, both, or neither) is worth a longer-horizon look.
- Follows the [[11-the-studio-experiential-learning-menu|Doc 11]] §6 privacy default if Shiloh wants it to go beyond family viewing.
