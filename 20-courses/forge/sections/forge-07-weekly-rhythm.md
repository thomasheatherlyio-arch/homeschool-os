## 7. Weekly Rhythm

Plugs into the normal [[04-student-dashboard|Doc 04]] rhythm, same as [[kitchen-course|Docs 14]]–16:

- **Monday planning:** one of the week's missions can be a Forge unit step — e.g., "train and log accuracy for this week's classifier" or "draft three prompt variations for the Week 5 illustration."
- **Sessions:** actual course time — reading, tool use, building, writing. Capture evidence same-day per [[04-student-dashboard|Doc 04]]'s "capture as you go" habit (a screenshot of the trained model, the written reflection, the draft).
- **Friday review:** log finished pieces into the Evidence Inbox, tag them to the right quadrant (Section 3) and subject (Section 4), and check whether the pace still fits.
