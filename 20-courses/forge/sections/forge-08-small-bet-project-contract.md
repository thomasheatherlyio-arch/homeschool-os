## 8. Small Bet & Project Contract

Per [[07-north-star-life-path|Doc 07]] §3, this starts as a small bet, not a locked-in 10-week commitment — and per [[04-student-dashboard|Doc 04]] §3, a project this size gets its own lightweight contract, modeled on [[00-governing-charter-and-toc|Doc 00]] §7.

**Suggested first small bet:** *"Run Weeks 1–3 (what AI is, training a classifier, building a small project with it) over the next 3–4 weeks. At the end, log it in the Interest Ledger honestly: is the technical side (how AI works, training models) actually interesting, or is the appeal mostly about the creative-production side coming later? Either answer is useful data for pacing the rest of the course."*

A project contract should cover:

- **What Shiloh commits to:** finishing each week's deliverable, being honest in the Week 6 self-audit and AI-use policy rather than treating it as a formality, and following the Section 10 account/supervision rules without exception.
- **What Thomas provides:** access to the tool accounts needed (Section 10), time in the weekly plan, and a real decision (per Section 9) on whether to invest in a paid Curious Refuge course beyond the free tier.
- **What "done" looks like per week:** the deliverable in the Section 5 table, actually finished and logged.
- **Review point:** end of Week 3 (the small-bet checkpoint above), and again at each Friday/Quarterly review after that.
