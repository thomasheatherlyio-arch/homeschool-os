## 3. Quadrant Map

| Component | Primary Quadrant ([[01-holistic-quadrants-the-circle|Doc 01]]) | Secondary |
|---|---|---|
| What AI actually is (Week 1) | 🧠 Mind & Mastery | — |
| Training a classifier (Weeks 2–3) | 🧠 Mind & Mastery | 🗂️ Systems & Self-Command |
| Generative text/image/audio (Weeks 4–5) | 🚀 Venture & Craft | 🧠 Mind & Mastery |
| AI ethics, bias & citizenship (Week 6) | 🕊️ Spirit & Character | 🧠 Mind & Mastery |
| AI & the future of work (Week 7) | 🧠 Mind & Mastery | 🚀 Venture & Craft |
| Curious Refuge content-production track (Weeks 8–9) | 🚀 Venture & Craft | 🧠 Mind & Mastery |
| Capstone — build, produce, present (Week 10) | 🚀 Venture & Craft | all five |

Like The Panel ([[panel-course|Doc 16]]), this course splits its weight almost evenly between **Mind & Mastery** (understanding how the technology actually works) and **Venture & Craft** (making something real with it) — with **Spirit & Character** getting a real, dedicated week for ethics that most of the other courses only touch in passing.
