# Doc 18 — The Forge: Introduction to AI & Applied AI Projects Course

**Purpose:** A 10-week, project-based elective introducing Shiloh to how artificial intelligence actually works and how to use it to make real things — built the same way [[kitchen-course|Docs 14]]–16 (The Kitchen, The Gym, The Panel) turned a real interest into a structured, self-paced curriculum that routes Texas-required and standard-practice subjects ([[03-academic-tracker-tx-compliance|Doc 03]]) through hands-on work. Where those courses each anchor in one craft (cooking, training, electrical), this one anchors in a tool that now touches almost every craft — so half the course is "how AI works" (foundations, ethics, citizenship) and half is "make something real with it" (a trained classifier, AI-generated writing/art/audio, and a capstone AI-assisted short film). Section 2 answers the specific research question this doc was built to settle: what already exists for teaching AI to kids, especially homeschoolers, and where the real gaps are. Section 9 covers Curious Refuge, the AI-filmmaking training platform Thomas asked to be included as a named curriculum option for the creative-production half of the course.
