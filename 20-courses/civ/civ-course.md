# Doc 19 — The Civ: History, Civics & Society through Civilization VI

**Purpose:** Shiloh has chosen to learn history, civics, and society by playing *Civilization VI* — not as a substitute for study, but as the hands-on workshop the same way [[kitchen-course|Doc 14]] turns cooking into a course and [[forge-course|Doc 18]] turns AI into a course. This doc builds that: a structured, self-paced curriculum that routes Texas-required **Good Citizenship** and standard-practice **History / Social Studies** ([[03-academic-tracker-tx-compliance|Doc 03]] §4) through a time-boxed campaign, a written Decision Log, real-history reading, and short outputs Thomas can actually check. Half the work is in the game (settling, governments, the civics tree, religion, trade, diplomacy, loyalty, Great People, wonders); the other half is holding the game up against real history — including what it simplifies or leaves out — and practicing civics in the real world, not only on a map.

**Status:** active (approved by Thomas 2026-11-22, [[20261122-civ-course]]). Start with Unit 0. *Civilization VI* access (already owned or a Dad-approved copy, including any expansions) was part of that sign-off.

## How a campaign works (read this with Section 4)

- **One guided campaign** is the through-line: [[campaign-1-log]]. Weekly units add reading, a short output, and a "Game vs. Reality" critique; they do not require restarting a new game every week. A short second game is optional if a unit needs a specific civ (for example, Greece) and Campaign 1 is in a different era.
- **Play is time-boxed to 45–60 minutes** per session. A play session **counts as course work only when** a Decision Log entry is written the same day (copy the block from [[civ-decision-log-template]] into [[campaign-1-log]]). Screenshots are optional; if one is taken, put it in `50-evidence/` and paste the path in the log.
- **War is not banned.** It is a history lesson: costs, warmongering, loyalty, and grievances (see Unit 0). For the first guided game, Shiloh picks a **non-domination** victory path and may still fight wars — he just has to log why, and what it cost.
- **"Civ checkpoint" routing.** When Campaign 1 changes era — Classical, Medieval, Renaissance, Industrial, Modern, or Atomic — Shiloh tells COLT: *"Civ checkpoint"* (or "I just entered the [era] era"). COLT reads [[campaign-1-log]] and asks **three Socratic real-history questions tied to Shiloh's actual in-game choices** (the government he adopted, a wonder he built, a war he started, a religion he founded, a deal he signed). COLT does not answer those questions for him. His answers are logged as evidence, `verified: pending`, for Thomas to check. *(Routing: say "Civ checkpoint" or "Civ session"; see `AGENTS.md`.)*
