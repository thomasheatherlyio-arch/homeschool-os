## 8. Capstone — Finish, Present, Reflect

**Required minimum:** Shiloh presents Campaign 1 to Thomas as a "Civ vs. History" talk — one civilization's path from the first settle through the latest era he reached — walking the real decisions in [[campaign-1-log]] (governments, wonders, wars, religion, trade, Great People) and comparing them, honestly, to what actually happened in that civilization's history (and to what the game left out). He also reads a short **personal civics statement**: rights and responsibilities he can name, and one way he practiced them (the interview, the city council meeting, or the museum / Capitol visit).

The campaign log is the paper trail. A few optional screenshots help; they do not replace the written record or the talk.

**Alternate capstone (Shiloh's choice, in place of a single-civ walkthrough):** a museum-style exhibit (poster, slides, or a short Studio video) organized by era, each panel pairing one in-game choice with one real-history source, plus the same personal civics statement. The standard does not change: he has to use *his* log, not a generic history report.

**Either version:**

- Gets logged in the Evidence Inbox ([[04-student-dashboard|Doc 04]] §2.3) with the finished piece and a short written reflection.
- Closes with an honest Interest Ledger entry ([[07-north-star-life-path|Doc 07]] §6) — confirmed / ruled out / still unclear — on whether history, civics, or both are worth a longer-horizon look (including a fuller Texas-history sequence if Thomas wants one later).
- Follows the [[11-the-studio-experiential-learning-menu|Doc 11]] §6 privacy default if Shiloh wants a video version to go beyond family viewing.
