## 6. Weekly Rhythm

Plugs into the normal [[04-student-dashboard|Doc 04]] rhythm, same as [[kitchen-course|Docs 14]]–16:

- **Monday planning:** one of the week's missions can be a Civ unit step — e.g., "Unit 0: write victory types in my own words and pick a non-domination path," or "Week 3: Rome reading + 45–60 min on Campaign 1 + Decision Log."
- **Sessions (time-boxed):** 45–60 minutes of play, plus the reading and the short output for that unit. Play **only counts** with a same-day entry copied from [[civ-decision-log-template]] into [[campaign-1-log]]. An optional screenshot goes in `50-evidence/` with the path pasted in the log.
- **"Civ checkpoint" (era change):** when Campaign 1 enters Classical, Medieval, Renaissance, Industrial, Modern, or Atomic, Shiloh tells COLT *"Civ checkpoint."* COLT reads the campaign log and asks three Socratic real-history questions tied to the actual choices in the log. Shiloh's answers are logged as pending evidence for Thomas. This can happen mid-week; it does not replace the Friday review.
- **Friday review:** log finished pieces into the Evidence Inbox, tag them to the right quadrant (Section 2) and subject (Section 3), and check whether the pace and the time-box still fit.
