## 2. Quadrant Map

| Component | Primary Quadrant ([[01-holistic-quadrants-the-circle|Doc 01]]) | Secondary |
|---|---|---|
| Unit 0 — victory types & early strategy | 🧠 Mind & Mastery | 🗂️ Systems & Self-Command |
| Early civilizations, Greece & Rome, trade, Middle Ages, exploration, industry, world wars (Weeks 1–7, 9–10) | 🧠 Mind & Mastery | — |
| Religion and society (Week 4) | 🧠 Mind & Mastery | 🕊️ Spirit & Character |
| Revolutions, the U.S. founding, Texas government (Week 8) | 🧠 Mind & Mastery | 🕊️ Spirit & Character |
| Modern civics, voting, rights & responsibilities, local government, civic actions (Week 11) | 🕊️ Spirit & Character | 🧠 Mind & Mastery |
| Decision Log, time-boxed sessions, era checkpoints, 12-week follow-through | 🗂️ Systems & Self-Command | 🧠 Mind & Mastery |
| Capstone — present, compare, reflect (Week 12) | 🚀 Venture & Craft | all five |

This course sits primarily in **Mind & Mastery** (world history, U.S. founding, reading and writing) with a real, required share for **Spirit & Character** (Good Citizenship lived out, not only described). **Systems & Self-Command** is how the campaign actually holds: one log, one time-box, no "I just played."
