## 7. Small Bet & Project Contract

Per [[07-north-star-life-path|Doc 07]] §3, this starts as a small bet, not a locked-in 12-week commitment — and per [[04-student-dashboard|Doc 04]] §3, a project this size gets its own lightweight contract, modeled on [[00-governing-charter-and-toc|Doc 00]] §7.

**Suggested first small bet:** *"Run Unit 0 plus Weeks 1–3 (victory types and early strategy, river valleys, Greece, Rome) over the next 3–4 weeks, with a Decision Log entry for every play session. At the end, log it in the Interest Ledger honestly: is the history and civics side actually interesting, or was the pull mostly the game — and did a non-domination victory path hold my attention, or did I drift back into only waging war? Either answer is useful data for pacing the rest of the course."*

A project contract should cover:

- **What Shiloh commits to:** finishing each unit's deliverable; keeping play sessions to 45–60 minutes; writing the Decision Log the same day (sessions without a log do not count); choosing a non-domination path for Campaign 1; logging wars honestly rather than hiding them or treating them as the only way to play; completing the two civic actions by Week 11.
- **What Thomas provides:** approval to start (this course is draft until he signs off); *Civilization VI* access (already owned or a Dad-approved copy, plus any expansions the units assume); time in the weekly plan; and verification of evidence, including Civ checkpoint answers and the capstone presentation.
- **What "done" looks like per week:** the deliverable in the Section 4 table, actually finished and logged, not just a longer play session.
- **Review point:** end of Week 3 (the small-bet checkpoint above), and again at each Friday / Quarterly review after that.
