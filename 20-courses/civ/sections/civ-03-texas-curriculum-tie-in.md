## 3. Texas Curriculum Tie-In (per [[03-academic-tracker-tx-compliance|Doc 03]])

The proposal ([[20261122-civ-course]]) asks Thomas to designate this course toward **History / Social Studies** and **Good Citizenship** once approved. Until he does, nothing is counted as complete. This table is the intended mapping, not a completed credit.

| Subject | TX status ([[03-academic-tracker-tx-compliance|Doc 03]] §1) | How The Civ covers it | Evidence type |
|---|---|---|---|
| **History / Social Studies** | Standard practice | World-history arc (river valleys through the 20th century) plus U.S. founding; Grade 7/8 Social Studies TEKS used as a scope check, not as a workbook to finish line-by-line (see [[civ-12-sources\|Sources]]) | Unit write-ups, maps, timelines, Game vs. Reality critiques, capstone presentation |
| **Good Citizenship** | Required | Constitutions, Bill of Rights, three branches and checks and balances, Texas government, voting, rights and responsibilities, local government; two real civic actions (interview; city council *or* museum/Capitol visit) | Reflections, civic-action notes, Week 8 mini-presentation to Dad, checkpoint answers |
| **Reading** | Required | Assigned Khan Academy / Crash Course / iCivics (or equivalent) reading or video-plus-notes each unit | Reading notes, source list in the Decision Log or unit write-up |
| **Spelling & Grammar** | Required | Decision Log entries, unit reflections, checkpoint answers, and the capstone write-up, in Shiloh's own words | Drafts and finished write-ups |
| **Executive Function** | Core to the plan ([[03-academic-tracker-tx-compliance|Doc 03]] §4) | 45–60 minute time-boxes, same-day logging, a multi-week campaign that only counts with written output | [[campaign-1-log]], weekly mission logs, Friday review notes |

This is **not** a complete standalone Grade 7 Texas History course. Week 8 and the civic actions cover Texas government and a hook into Texas history; they do not replace a full Texas-history sequence if Thomas later wants one. Log every right-hand-column item into [[03-academic-tracker-tx-compliance|Doc 03]]'s Evidence Log as it's produced — same discipline as The Kitchen, The Gym, The Panel, and The Forge.
