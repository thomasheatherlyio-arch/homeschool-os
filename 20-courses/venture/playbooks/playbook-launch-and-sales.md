# Playbook 8: Launch & First-Sale Execution

Linked to: [[playbooks-index]] | [[playbook-persona-creation]] | [[shiloh-hq-product-hunt-launch-notes]] | [[playbook-digital-presence]] | [[shiloh-playbook]]

## Purpose
Execute a compliant, high-craft Product Hunt launch (hosted by Thomas) and close our first paying US homeschool customers without spam or rule violations.

## When to Use
- Phase 5–7 once the sanitized MVP, interactive demo, and checkout are live and tested.

## Part A: Product Hunt Launch Checklist (Via Thomas's Personal Account)
*Full rules & citations in [[shiloh-hq-product-hunt-launch-notes]].*
1. **T-21 to T-7 Days (Account & Media)**:
   - **Product Hunt maker profile is Thomas only**: his **personal** PH account (>1 week old, verified email, real name/photo, active in Forums/comments). Do **not** create a PH account for Shiloh or for the persona (16+ rule, and PH bans company/fake accounts).
   - In the launch description and **Maker Comment**, credit Thomas as the adult maker/cofounder and the approved [[playbook-persona-creation|persona]] as the teen Student-CEO cofounder. Never use Shiloh's real name, face, location, school, or personal accounts.
   - Prepare assets: Name (<=40 chars), Tagline (<=60 chars), Description (<=500 chars), Logo (240x240px), >=3 screenshots (1270x760px), 60–90s **faceless** demo (captions or Dad narration unless a real-voice exception is signed off), **live no-signup interactive web demo link**, up to 3 tags, PH promo code (e.g., `PHFOUNDER` for $10 off).
2. **T-3 Days (Privacy & Dad Sign-Off Gate)**:
   - Thomas audits every screenshot, video frame, Maker Comment line, and demo page for real-name/face/location/school leaks, Texas/calendar data, and personal accounts, then signs off.
   - Pre-schedule email to waitlist + prep social posts (Thomas + persona channels only).
3. **Launch Day (12:01 AM PT Start)**:
   - Thomas posts the launch + Maker First Comment from **his** profile (who we are: Thomas + persona, why we built it, who it's for, 1 specific feedback question).
   - Share link with waitlist, beta testers, and communities where Thomas is already active—**ask for feedback & demo tries, NEVER upvotes**.
   - Respond fast to every comment throughout the day (Shiloh drafts in the persona's public voice; Thomas reviews and posts).

## Part B: First-Sale Tactics (Beyond Launch Day)
1. **Warm Beta-to-Paid Conversion**: Follow up with the 5–10 interview parents and 2 beta families: offer Founding Member pricing ($29) + 15-min setup help in exchange for an honest testimonial.
2. **Value-First Homeschool Community Posts (Dad-Led)**: Post a genuinely useful breakdown (*"How we stopped nagging over daily homeschool work using a 60/40 board + Socratic AI check-in"*) in 2–3 vetted homeschool parent forums/groups where self-promotion rules allow links in comments/bio.
3. **Direct Helpful Outreach**: When a parent on X/Reddit/Facebook publicly asks *"How do you keep a 14-year-old organized in homeschool?"*, Thomas replies with real advice first + optional link to the free interactive demo.
4. **Offer & Pricing Experiments (<$25)**:
   - Test 1 variable per week: (a) $29 Founding Kit vs. $49 Standard, or (b) "Free Lite Starter -> $39 Full 15-Skill Pro Kit" vs. direct paid checkout, or (c) $149 Guided Setup upsell.

## Privacy & Dad Sign-Off Gate (Launch Day)
- [ ] Product Hunt submitted strictly from Thomas's personal maker account (no persona or minor PH account)
- [ ] Maker Comment and page copy credit Thomas + the approved persona only (no real teen identity)
- [ ] Interactive live web demo works cleanly in incognito browser (zero login wall, zero PII)
- [ ] Zero "ask for upvotes" language anywhere in emails, DMs, or posts
- [ ] Checkout test purchase ($1 test or 100% coupon) verified end-to-end by Thomas
- [ ] **Privacy & Dad Sign-Off Gate + final launch sign-off recorded**
