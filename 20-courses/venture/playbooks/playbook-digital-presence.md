# Playbook 4: Digital Presence & Brand Kit

Linked to: [[playbooks-index]] | [[playbook-persona-creation]] | [[shiloh-hq-product-hunt-launch-notes]] | [[shiloh-playbook]]

## Purpose
Establish a clean, credible online home (product name, domain, landing page, email list, and parent-owned social channels) in one weekend for under $25—using only the approved [[playbook-persona-creation|persona]] for the teen cofounder and zero personal details in the brand.

## When to Use
- After MVP validation passes (Phase 4) and before pre-launch content starts.

## Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Lock the Persona First**: Complete [[playbook-persona-creation]] (handle, visual mode, bio, Dad sign-off) before buying a domain or drafting public copy. Do not use the real teen's name in the product, domain, or handles.
2. **Pick a Product Name (3 Candidates)**:
   - Brand and domain should be the *product* (and optionally the approved persona handle), never a personal name. Good patterns: *HomeschoolOS*, *StudentCEO*, *ForgeVault*, *StudyCommand*.
   - Check domain availability (`.com` or `.co`, <$15/yr) and quick USPTO trademark search on the **brand/persona name, not a personal name** ([[playbook-legal-setup]]).
3. **Account Ownership Check (Age Gate — See [[shiloh-hq-product-hunt-launch-notes]])**:
   - **16+ / 18+ Platforms (Thomas owns & registers in his legal name or the business)**: Domain registrar, Hosting/Cloudflare/Vercel, Product Hunt (16+, Thomas's *personal* maker profile—not a persona or brand account), LinkedIn (16+), Substack (16+), Gumroad/Stripe/Lemon Squeezy payouts.
   - **Brand / persona channels (Thomas holds login, recovery email, and approves every post)**: X, YouTube (AdSense guardian 18+), Instagram, TikTok, and `hello@yourdomain.com` (or `persona@yourdomain.com`). These are **separate from anyone's personal accounts**.
4. **Create a Minimal 30-Minute Brand Kit**:
   - 2 colors (1 primary, 1 accent), 1 clean font pair, 1 square 240x240px product icon/logo, the approved persona avatar (illustrated or abstract—never a photo of the real teen), and 1 one-line tagline (<=60 chars).
5. **Build a High-Converting Landing Page (Single Goal)**:
   - **Above the fold**: Clear headline (outcome for parent + teen), 1-sentence subhead, interactive demo button + buy/waitlist CTA, clean dashboard screenshot (sample data only).
   - **Body**: 3 core pain-to-solution blocks; a founder blurb that names **Thomas** as the adult cofounder and the **approved persona** as the teen Student-CEO (no real name, face, school, or location); FAQ (Mac/Windows, AI cost, privacy/local-first); transparent pricing; footer Terms & Privacy Policy.
6. **Set Up Email List (Asset Dad Owns)**:
   - Connect a free/low-cost list (Buttondown, Kit free tier, or Thomas's Substack) with double opt-in and a parent-held brand inbox (`hello@yourdomain.com`), never a personal email.
7. **Privacy & Dad Sign-Off Gate (Mandatory)**:
   - WHOIS privacy on; no personal phone/home address in the footer (use a business alias/PO box if required by CAN-SPAM—check with Dad); zero real name, face, location, school, personal accounts, or voice-identifying audio unless Dad has signed off.

## Digital Presence Launch Checklist
- [ ] [[playbook-persona-creation|Persona]] handle, avatar, and bio approved; brand email/socials held by Dad
- [ ] Product/brand name (not a personal name) checked against USPTO & domain <$15/yr registered by Thomas
- [ ] All 16+/18+ and brand accounts created & owned by Thomas (documented in family password manager)
- [ ] Landing page live with working Demo CTA + Checkout/Waitlist CTA + Terms/Privacy footer; founder copy uses Thomas + persona only
- [ ] Email capture tested end-to-end (subscribe -> welcome email received)
- [ ] **Privacy & Dad Sign-Off Gate passed** (no real name, face, location, school, or personal accounts)
