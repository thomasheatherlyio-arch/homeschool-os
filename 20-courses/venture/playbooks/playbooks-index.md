## Reusable Persona Creation Step
**Run [[playbook-persona-creation]] before Playbooks 4, 7, and 8** (and again for a new venture only if Dad approves a fresh handle). The teen cofounder appears in public only as a Dad-approved persona: separate handle/name, illustrated or faceless visuals, no real name/face/location/school/personal accounts, and all brand email/accounts held by Dad. Reuse the same approved persona across products unless there is a clear reason to change it.
