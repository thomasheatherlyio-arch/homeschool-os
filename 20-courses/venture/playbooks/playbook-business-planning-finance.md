# Playbook 5: Business Planning & Finance

Linked to: [[playbooks-index]] | [[shiloh-hq-product-hunt-launch-notes]] | [[shiloh-playbook]]

## Purpose
Understand how a digital business actually makes money: 1-page Lean Canvas, smart pricing, unit economics, simple bookkeeping, and real teen finance lessons—using real numbers only.

## When to Use
- Before setting a price, opening a checkout page, or spending any money beyond $25.
- Monthly to update real P&L numbers.

## Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Fill Out the 1-Page Lean Canvas** (Shiloh drafts in 20 mins): Problem, Customer Segments, Unique Value Prop, Solution, Channels, Revenue Streams, Cost Structure, Key Metrics, Unfair Advantage.
2. **Design 2–3 Pricing Tiers (Value-Based, Not Cost-Plus)**:
   - Anchor with a higher-touch tier (e.g., $149 Guided Setup) so the $39–$49 Pro Kit feels like a steal; offer a $29 Founding Member launch promo.
3. **Calculate Unit Economics Before Selling**:
   - Example on Gumroad ($39 Pro Kit):
     - Gross Price: **$39.00**
     - Platform/Payment Fees (~10% Gumroad + processing, verify current rate at launch): **~$3.90–$4.20**
     - Variable Delivery Cost (static files / web demo on free tier): **~$0.00–$0.50**
     - **Contribution Margin**: **~$34.50 (~88%)** per sale!
   - *Break-even check*: If fixed startup cost = $14 domain + $10 smoke test = **$24**, **1 single sale at $29–$39 makes the venture profitable!**
4. **Set Up Simple Bookkeeping (Parent-Controlled)**:
   - Thomas tracks all venture inflows/outflows in a separate ledger/sub-account (never mix with personal allowance).
   - Log every receipt, fee, refund, and experiment cost (<$25 cap per test without new proposal).
5. **Apply the Kid-Friendly Profit Split & Tax Rule** (pre-agreed in family charter):
   - Every $100 of *Net Profit* (Revenue minus Costs): e.g., **25% Tax Reserve** (held by Dad), **25% Reinvestment Fund** (tools/domains/ads), **50% Founder Pay / Savings** (earned pay for real work done—note tax rules in [[playbook-legal-setup]]).

## Kid-Friendly Money Lessons (Discuss Each Month)
- **Revenue != Profit**: Why $1,000 in sales with $950 in ads is worse than $150 in sales with $15 in expenses.
- **Gross Margin**: Why digital products (~85–95% margin) beat physical inventory for teen founders.
- **CAC vs. LTV**: How much it costs to win 1 customer vs. what they spend over time.
