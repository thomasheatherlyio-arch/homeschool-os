# Playbook 9: Metrics & Weekly Retros

Linked to: [[playbooks-index]] | [[shiloh-hq-venture-dashboard]] | [[shiloh-playbook]]

## Purpose
Replace guesswork and vanity metrics with **real numbers** and a fast Friday retrospective that turns every win or flop into compounding founder judgment.

## When to Use
- Every Friday during the Venture block (aligned with `friday-review`).
- At the end of every launch or <$25 weekend experiment.

## Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Track the 5 Funnel Numbers (Real Numbers Only — Never Estimates)**:
   - **Reach**: Unique visitors to landing page / demo this week.
   - **Engagement**: Visitors who clicked the Interactive Demo or signed up for email (`%` conversion).
   - **Intent**: Checkout button clicks (`%` of visitors).
   - **Sales**: Paid orders & Net Revenue after fees/refunds.
   - **Retention / Proof**: Beta/buyer setup completions, support tickets, or honest testimonials collected.
2. **Log Every Experiment Result**: Did our <$25 hypothesis pass or fail its pre-set bar?
3. **Make One Weekly Call (Kill, Fix, or Double Down)**:
   - High traffic, low demo clicks -> **Fix headline/positioning**.
   - High demo clicks, low checkout clicks -> **Fix offer/price/trust or onboarding fear**.
   - Good conversion, low traffic -> **Double down on distribution/content**.
   - Repeated <5% interest after 2 fixes -> **Kill or pivot the idea** (using [[playbook-next-product-reuse]]).
