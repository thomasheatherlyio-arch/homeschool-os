# Playbook: Persona Creation (Reusable Public Identity)

Linked to: [[playbooks-index]] | [[shiloh-hq-venture-brief]] | [[playbook-digital-presence]] | [[playbook-content-founder-influencer]] | [[playbook-legal-setup]] | [[shiloh-playbook]]

## Purpose
Create a public cofounder identity (a **persona**) so a teen can build in public and appear in brand copy **without** putting their real name, face, location, school, or personal accounts on the product.

## What a Persona Is
A persona is a **separate public builder identity**—a handle, look, and on-brand voice the audience meets. It is not a fake adult, not a legal person, and not a substitute for Dad-owned accounts. Inside the vault we still use real names; outside the vault, only the approved persona (plus Thomas, where an adult legal identity is required) appears.

## When to Use
- Before any landing page, social bio, Product Hunt copy, or content goes public.
- At the start of every new venture if you want a fresh handle, or reuse the existing approved persona (do not invent a new one casually).

## Rules (Non-Negotiable)
- **Handle / display name**: Shiloh proposes 3 options; **Dad approves one**. It must not be the real first or last name, a nickname family/friends would recognize, or a hint at school, city, or personal accounts.
- **No personal details in the brand**: no real name, face, location, school/co-op, or personal emails/handles. Exact age is optional and only if Dad signs off (default public language: "a homeschooled teen Student-CEO").
- **Voice & likeness**: no real face, and no **voice-identifying** audio, unless Dad signs off case by case. Default to illustrated/avatar, logo-as-character, or fully faceless screen + typography/captions (Dad can narrate if a voice is needed).
- **Accounts & email**: all persona/brand emails and socials are **separate from personal accounts** and **held by Dad** (password manager, recovery email, 16+/18+ where required). The persona never gets its own Product Hunt, Stripe, or other adult-gated account.

## Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Shiloh drafts 3 handle + one-line bio options** (what the teen cofounder is *for*, not who they are in real life).
2. **Dad reviews and approves one**, plus the visual mode: illustrated avatar, abstract character, or faceless-only.
3. **Build a one-page Persona Sheet** (kept private in the vault; only approved lines are copied to public pages): handle, one-line bio, visual mode, do/don't list, and the brand email Dad will create.
4. **Dad creates the brand inbox and any socials** in the product or persona name (never a personal Gmail/X/YouTube). Document ownership in the family password manager.
5. **Run the Privacy & Dad Sign-Off Gate** before the handle, avatar, or bio appears on a domain, landing page, or post.

## Persona Sheet + Gate Checklist
- [ ] Handle is not derived from real name, school, or city; Dad approved
- [ ] Visual: illustrated / avatar / faceless (no photos of the real teen)
- [ ] Default content is silent or Dad-narrated; real voice only with written sign-off
- [ ] Brand email + socials are separate and held by Dad
- [ ] Public copy never pairs identifying details (real name, face, school, location, personal accounts)
- [ ] **Privacy & Dad Sign-Off Gate passed**
