# Playbook 7: Content Development & Founder-Influencer

Linked to: [[playbooks-index]] | [[playbook-persona-creation]] | [[shiloh-hq-product-hunt-launch-notes]] | [[shiloh-playbook]]

## Purpose
Build an authentic "Headmaster Dad + teen Student-CEO persona" audience in public—driving trust, Product Hunt momentum, and sales—while keeping Shiloh's real name, face, location, school, and personal accounts entirely off the brand.

## When to Use
- Weekly during Phase 5–8 (2–4 weeks *before* Product Hunt launch and ongoing afterward).

## Core Story & 4 Content Pillars
Tell the father + teen-builder story through **Thomas (named adult)** and the approved [[playbook-persona-creation|persona]] (public teen cofounder). Never "out" the real teen in a post, caption, filename, or thumbnail.
1. **Build-in-Public Demos (40%)**: Faceless screen-recordings of real features (60/40 priority board, Readiness Examiner, AI Socratic coaching vs. cheating), with captions or Dad narration unless a real-voice exception is signed off.
2. **Dad + Persona Co-Founder Lessons (25%)**: How Thomas coaches without doing the work; disagreeing on pricing; what failed in a <$25 experiment—written in Thomas's voice and/or the persona's approved public voice.
3. **Homeschool Systems & AI Literacy (25%)**: How teens can use AI agents (Grok Build / Cursor / Obsidian) to *learn deeper*, not skip thinking.
4. **Real Numbers Retros (10%)**: Honest metrics—even when sales = $0 or a test flops ([[shiloh-playbook]] "real numbers").

## Platform Strategy & Age Compliance
- **Thomas is the public adult (16+/18+ platforms)**: Thomas's own LinkedIn, Substack, and Product Hunt *personal maker* profile; he leads parent-community threads and posts or approves every word.
- **Persona channels, Dad-held**: Any X/YouTube/Instagram/TikTok or newsletter presence for the teen cofounder uses the approved persona handle and a brand inbox Dad owns. Shiloh drafts; he does not hold logins or reply to strangers.
- **Shiloh's builder craft (behind the persona)**: Writes drafts, records faceless screen captures, designs the illustrated avatar, and learns copywriting & editing. No face, and no voice-identifying audio, unless Dad signs off.

## Sustainable Weekly Cadence (Fits 4–6 hr Venture Block)
- **Tuesday (45 mins)**: Ship 1 short screen-demo video (60–90s) or annotated screenshot thread showing what we built this week.
- **Friday (30 mins)**: Turn the weekly retro into 1 honest build-in-public post (*"Week X: What we tested, real numbers, 1 lesson"*).
- **1x / 2 weeks**: Thomas publishes a deeper Substack/LinkedIn story for homeschool parents & builders.

## Privacy & Minor Online Safety Gate (NON-NEGOTIABLE)
Before ANY post, video, screenshot, or bio is published:
- [ ] **Persona only**: Public attribution is Thomas + the approved persona handle. No real name (first or last), face, location, school/co-op, or personal accounts.
- [ ] **Visual default is illustrated, avatar, or faceless**: No photos or on-camera appearance, and no voice-identifying audio, unless Dad signs off in writing for that asset.
- [ ] **No personal accounts / open DMs**: Shiloh never holds brand logins or replies to strangers; Thomas owns/moderates every channel and inbox.
- [ ] **PII scrub on screenshots**: Inspect every frame for browser tabs, emails, bookmarks, real names, or personal vault filenames.
- [ ] **Thomas Sign-Off Recorded** before publishing.

## AI-Agent Prompt (Coach Mode)
> "You are my Content & Storytelling Coach per [[shiloh-playbook]] and [[playbook-persona-creation]]. Help me turn this week's real work (`<WHAT WE BUILT OR LEARNED>`) into 1 build-in-public post and 1 60-second faceless screen-demo script attributed to our approved persona (and Thomas where the adult cofounder should speak). Ask me for the real numbers and 1 honest mistake first—do NOT invent fake hype. Before finalizing, run the Privacy Gate and flag any real name, face, voice, location, school, or personal account."
