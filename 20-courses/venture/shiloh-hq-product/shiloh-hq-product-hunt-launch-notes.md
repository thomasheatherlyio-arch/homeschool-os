# Product Hunt, Payment & Platform Age Rules (Cited 2026 Research)

Linked to: [[shiloh-hq-venture-dashboard]] | [[playbook-launch-and-sales]] | [[playbook-persona-creation]] | [[playbook-legal-setup]] | [[shiloh-playbook]]

> **Key Reality Check for a Teen Co-Founder**: Several major launch, payment, and creator platforms set minimum ages at **16+ or 18+**. Thomas (Dad) MUST hold those accounts legally. Shiloh leads product craft behind an approved [[playbook-persona-creation|persona]]; none of his personal details (name, face, location, school, accounts) are part of the brand.

---

## 1. Product Hunt Rules & Launch Process (2026)

### Age & Account Rules (Critical Flag)
- **Minimum Age is 16+**: Product Hunt's Terms of Service explicitly state: *"The Services are not intended for children below 16 and we do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone under the age of 16 or knowingly allow such persons to register with the Services."* ([Product Hunt Legal / ToS](https://www.producthunt.com/legal)).
  - **Action**: At age 14, **Shiloh CANNOT create or hold a Product Hunt account**, and the persona must not have one either (PH requires a real personal account; brand/fake accounts cannot post). **Thomas is the sole PH account holder, submitter, and maker profile.** Credit the approved persona as the teen Student-CEO cofounder in the Maker Comment and launch copy only—never Shiloh's real name, face, location, school, or personal accounts.
- **Personal Account Required (No Brand Accounts)**: Only personal accounts (full real name, personal photo, bio) can submit, comment, or vote; company/domain-named accounts are blocked from posting ([Personal vs Company Account](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/771527-personal-account-vs-company-account)).
- **1-Week Minimum Account Age & Verified Email**: A personal account must have a verified email and be **at least 1 week old** before submitting a launch (unless granted early access via newsletter, though 2–4 weeks of genuine commenting/forum activity beforehand is best) ([How to get access to post](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/481909-how-can-i-get-access-to-post), [How Product Hunt works](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/17734306-1-how-product-hunt-works)).

### Featuring Rules (Why We Must Ship a Live Interactive Companion!)
- **What Gets Featured**: Live, working digital products that score high on *Useful, Novel, High Craft, or Creative* ([Featuring Guidelines](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/9883485-product-hunt-featuring-guidelines)).
- **What Does NOT Get Featured**: Product Hunt excludes waitlists (unless immediate access is given), plain documents/blogs, generic templates, and standalone courses/services without a live product experience ([What kind of products get featured](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/17734341-2-what-kind-of-products-get-featured)).
  - **Design Implication**: Do **NOT** launch just a static Obsidian `.zip` folder! Pair the Obsidian vault kit with a **live, zero-signup interactive web demo** (e.g., interactive Command Center + Readiness Diagnostic preview + Vault Configurator built in Cursor) so visitors can *use* the product immediately in their browser.

### Launch Specs & Promotion Rules
- **Timing**: Launches go live at **12:01 AM Pacific Time** on the scheduled date ([How Product Hunt works](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/17734306-1-how-product-hunt-works)). Pick a day when Thomas and Shiloh can actively monitor and reply ([Set goals & launch date](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/17734355-3-set-your-goals-and-pick-your-launch-date)).
- **Page Specs** ([Build your product page](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/17834537-4-build-your-product-page)):
  - Name: <= 40 chars (no emojis/taglines)
  - Tagline: <= 60 chars (clear > clever; skip "#1" or "best")
  - Description: <= 500 chars (problem -> value -> features)
  - Logo: 240x240px (JPG/PNG/GIF <= 2MB)
  - Media: >= 1 screenshot (1270x760px recommended) + short **faceless** screen-demo (captions or Dad narration unless a real-voice exception is signed off) + clickable demo link
  - Tags (up to 3), Pricing tier, optional PH Promo Code, and **Maker First Comment** (who we are, who it's for, ask a feedback question).
- **Hard Promotion Rules** ([How to promote your launch](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/17734384-5-how-to-promote-your-launch-and-what-to-avoid)):
  - **NEVER ask for upvotes** (ask for *feedback, comments, and trying the demo*).
  - No upvote-gated giveaways, no paid/bought engagement, no spam DMs, no extra/fake accounts.
- **Relaunches**: Allowed only for major new features, platforms, redesigns, or pivots (requires a relaunch request if within 2 months of prior launch) ([Relaunch your product](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/17734411-7-relaunch-your-product)).

## 2. Payment Processor & Payout Age Rules

| Platform | Minimum Account Age | Under-18 Payout / Representative Rule | Recommended Setup for Shiloh + Thomas | Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stripe** | **13+** | Users under 18 **must add an adult Representative** (parent/legal guardian) who binds to the agreement and assumes full legal responsibility and liability. | Thomas acts as adult Representative (or owns the business account directly). | [Stripe Services Agreement §1.2(b)](https://stripe.com/legal/ssa) |
| **Gumroad** | **13+** | In the **US**, sellers aged 13–17 can sell and receive payouts by adding a **Legal Guardian** in Payout Settings (guardian's legal name, DOB, address, SSN/ID, and guardian's own acceptance of payment-partner terms). | **Easiest MVP checkout**: Fast setup, handles digital delivery + US guardian payouts cleanly. Thomas completes guardian verification. | [Gumroad Payout Settings](https://gumroad.com/help/article/260-your-payout-settings-page), [Gumroad ToS](https://gumroad.com/terms) |
| **Lemon Squeezy** | **18+ practical** (ID verification) | Requires merchant KYC identity verification with a **government-issued photo ID** before selling; no published under-18 guardian onboarding process. | **Thomas (18+) owns and verifies the account** if used as Merchant of Record. | [Lemon Squeezy Verify Identity](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/verify-your-identity), [Lemon Squeezy Terms](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/terms) |
