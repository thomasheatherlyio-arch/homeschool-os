## Activity 1 — Score it yourself (Mon, 60m)
Use [[playbook-idea-research-scorecard]]. Write your own 1–5 scores and a one-sentence evidence reason for each criterion. Cap any score at 3 if you have no real evidence. Then compare your total to the **reference score of 80/100** in the playbook (that reference is a draft, not your grade).

- **You decide:** each raw score, the total, and the Top 3 risky assumptions.
- **Dad verifies:** challenges any 4 or 5 and asks you to defend it out loud.
- **Evidence to log:** `50-evidence/2026-Q4/<YYYY-MM-DD>-venture-scorecard-homeschool-os.md` — link [[shiloh-hq-phase-1-scorecard]]; proof is the filled table + your comparison notes.
- **Success criteria:** All 6 criteria scored with written reasons; total calculated correctly; comparison to 80/100 written (what you scored higher/lower and why); Top 3 assumptions listed; `verified: pending`.

## Activity 2 — Problem statement & customer persona (Tue, 45m)
Write **one sentence** in this shape: *"[Specific buyer] struggles with [specific painful problem], so we give them [specific product] to get [measurable outcome]."* Then write a **customer persona** for a US homeschool *parent* (the buyer)—not the public cofounder persona.

- **You decide:** the sentence and the parent persona (goals, pains, tools they already tried, what would make them pay).
- **Dad verifies:** checks that the buyer is the parent (not the teen) and that the sentence is specific, not hype.
- **Evidence to log:** `50-evidence/2026-Q4/<YYYY-MM-DD>-venture-problem-customer-persona.md` — link [[shiloh-hq-phase-1-problem-customer]].
- **Success criteria:** One sentence, no jargon/hype; parent persona has pains, current tools, and a willingness-to-pay note; `verified: pending`.

## Activity 3 — Interview list & Mom Test prep (Wed, 75m)
Build a list of **10 potential US homeschool parent interviewees**. Names and contact paths come **through Dad only**. You may suggest people Dad already knows (co-op friends, church, relatives' parents)—you do **not** DM, email, or post from your own accounts, and nobody is invited this week.

Prep questions from the Mom Test script in [[playbook-customer-discovery-validation]] (no "would you buy this?" questions).

- **You decide:** who to suggest, which 8 questions to use, and what you most want to learn.
- **Dad verifies:** vets the list, holds all contact info, and strikes any leading questions. He does not send invites until a later week he approves.
- **Evidence to log:** `50-evidence/2026-Q4/<YYYY-MM-DD>-venture-interview-list-prep.md` — link [[shiloh-hq-phase-1-interview-list]].
- **Success criteria:** 10 rows with a Dad-sourced path for each; zero outreach sent; 8+ non-leading questions written; `verified: pending`.

## Activity 4 — Smoke-test copy & <$25 budget (Thu, 60m)
Draft landing-page smoke-test copy and an itemized budget of **$25 or less**. This is a **plan and draft only**—do not buy a domain, publish a page, or open brand accounts this week.

Follow the **proposed** public-identity rule in [[shiloh-hq-venture-brief]] (it counts only after Dad approves [[20261124-venture-shiloh-hq-product]]): copy may use Thomas as the adult cofounder and a placeholder / proposed [[playbook-persona-creation|brand persona]] ("a homeschooled teen Student-CEO" or `[Persona handle TBD]`). No real name, face, location, school, or personal accounts.

- **You decide:** headline, subhead, CTA (`Notify me` / `Reserve at $29`), and how to spend $0–$25 later if Dad approves.
- **Dad verifies:** privacy gate on the draft (no personal details) and that nothing went live.
- **Evidence to log:** `50-evidence/2026-Q4/<YYYY-MM-DD>-venture-smoke-test-draft.md` — link [[shiloh-hq-phase-1-smoke-test-draft]].
- **Success criteria:** Headline + subhead + CTA + 3 benefit lines drafted; budget itemized and <=$25; persona/privacy check passed; **no URL published**; `verified: pending`.

## Activity 5 — Friday retro (Fri, 45m)

- **You decide:** what worked, what flopped, and a single next-week priority (still no publishing unless Dad opens a new proposal).
- **Dad verifies:** runs `verify-evidence` on this week's pending notes (2-minute check each).
- **Evidence to log:** `50-evidence/2026-Q4/<YYYY-MM-DD>-venture-phase-1-retro.md` — link [[shiloh-hq-phase-1-retro]].
