# Venture Roadmap: Homeschool OS (8 Phases)

Linked to: [[shiloh-hq-venture-dashboard]] | [[shiloh-hq-venture-brief]] | [[shiloh-hq-phase-1-kickoff]] | [[playbooks-index]] | [[playbook-persona-creation]] | [[shiloh-playbook]]

## Realistic Pacing for a 14-Year-Old Student-CEO
- **Weekly Time Budget**: **4–6 hours/week** inside the Venture course block (protects the 60% core academic block).
- **Suggested Weekly Cadence**:
  - **Mon (30m)**: Pick 1 weekly Venture deliverable & <$25 experiment (align with `monday-plan`).
  - **Tue–Thu (2–3 hrs total)**: Customer interviews, Cursor/Grok Build sprints, or content drafting.
  - **Fri (45m)**: Log real numbers + run [[playbook-metrics-and-retros|Friday Venture Retro]] (`friday-review`) + submit any `proposals/` for Dad sign-off.
  - **Weekend (Optional 1–2 hrs)**: Run <$25 smoke tests or live demos with Dad.

---

## Phase 1: Discover & Score (Weeks 1–2)
- **Week 1 kickoff**: [[shiloh-hq-phase-1-kickoff]] (`status: draft` — starts only after Thomas approves [[20261124-venture-shiloh-hq-product]]; do not ship or publish).
- **Playbook**: [[playbook-idea-research-scorecard]] (plus [[playbook-customer-discovery-validation]] prep)
- **Shiloh's Activities**: Audit our existing 15 skills and dashboard; research 5 homeschool competitors/alternatives (Notion homeschool templates, Trello, Outschool, Khanmigo); **score the idea himself** on the Venture Scorecard and compare to the reference 80/100; write a one-sentence problem and a parent customer persona; build a 10-person interview list sourced through Dad (no outreach from his accounts) with Mom Test prep; draft smoke-test copy and a <$25 budget (brand persona only, not live); Friday retro.
- **Deliverables**: Self-scored Venture Scorecard + comparison notes, problem sentence + parent persona, 10 Dad-sourced interview leads + script, smoke-test draft + budget, Friday retro and evidence notes.
- **Dad's Role**: Approve the proposal before Week 1 starts; challenge optimistic scores; source/vet interviewees and hold contacts; privacy-gate the draft copy; verify evidence. No publishing this week.

## Phase 2: Validate (Weeks 3–4)
- **Playbook**: [[playbook-customer-discovery-validation]]
- **Shiloh's Activities**: Draft 8 non-leading interview questions; sit in on (or lead with Dad) 5–10 interviews with US homeschool parents; launch a <$25 weekend smoke-test landing page measuring email signups + "Notify me at $39" clicks.
- **Deliverables**: 5–10 interview notes (pain points in parents' exact words), Smoke Test conversion metrics (>=15% email signup or >=3 pre-intent commitments).
- **Dad's Role**: Source/vet homeschool parent interviewees; attend calls; approve and host smoke-test page.

## Phase 3: Prototype & Sanitize MVP (Weeks 5–7)
- **Playbook**: [[playbook-prototype-cursor]]
- **Shiloh's Activities**: Clone vault structure into a clean public-safe repo; strip all PII, Texas/family specifics, and personal calendar data; generalize Readiness Examiner banks; build a **live interactive web demo / setup wizard** (so Product Hunt visitors can test the Command Center & Readiness Examiner in-browser without installing Obsidian first); run a 15-minute install test with 2 beta families.
- **Deliverables**: Sanitized `homeschool-os-starter` repo/zip, Live Interactive Web Demo URL, 2 beta tester feedback logs, Privacy Audit Checklist passed.
- **Dad's Role**: Technical/security code review; verify zero personal data leaks; recruit 2 beta families.

## Phase 4: Brand, Finance & Legal Setup (Weeks 8–9)
- **Playbooks**: [[playbook-persona-creation]], [[playbook-digital-presence]], [[playbook-business-planning-finance]], [[playbook-legal-setup]]
- **Shiloh's Activities**: Complete the persona sheet (3 handle options + illustrated/faceless visual); pick 3 product name candidates and check domain/USPTO availability on the **brand/persona**, not a personal name; draft the 1-page Lean Canvas, pricing tiers, and simple P&L sheet; draft plain-English EULA/Privacy notes for Dad's review.
- **Deliverables**: Approved persona (handle, avatar, bio), final product name & domain (<$15), Lean Canvas + Unit Economics sheet, Gumroad/Stripe product draft, Privacy/EULA/COPPA checklist.
- **Dad's Role**: Approve the persona; register domain; create and hold all brand email/socials and 18+ payout/legal accounts; review legal/COPPA/EULA and trademark-search the brand (not a personal name).

## Phase 5: Content & Launch Prep (Weeks 10–11)
- **Playbooks**: [[playbook-content-founder-influencer]], [[playbook-launch-and-sales]], [[shiloh-hq-product-hunt-launch-notes]]
- **Shiloh's Activities**: Record a 90-second **faceless** screen-capture demo (captions or Dad narration; no real face or voice-identifying audio unless Dad signs off); capture 4 clean screenshots (1270x760px) and 240x240px logo + persona avatar; draft PH Name/Tagline/Description/Maker Comment crediting Thomas + the persona; prep 5 build-in-public posts.
- **Deliverables**: Complete PH Launch Media Kit, 2-week pre-launch content queue, **Privacy & Dad Sign-Off Gate passed**.
- **Dad's Role**: Warm up **his personal** Product Hunt maker account (>=1 week old, active comments/forums); final Privacy Gate sign-off on all copy/media (persona only, no personal details); schedule launch date.

## Phase 6: Product Hunt Launch (Week 12)
- **Playbooks**: [[playbook-launch-and-sales]], [[shiloh-hq-product-hunt-launch-notes]]
- **Shiloh's Activities**: Co-draft real-time replies to PH comments (posted by Thomas or via Dad-reviewed script); monitor demo uptime and bug reports; log hourly traffic, demo clicks, and signups.
- **Deliverables**: Live PH launch at 12:01 AM PT, Launch Day Log, list of top 10 feature/feedback requests.
- **Dad's Role**: Submit launch from Thomas's personal PH maker profile; post Maker comment crediting the persona as teen cofounder; manage all live PH/LinkedIn/Substack replies; enforce no-upvote-asking rule and persona privacy.

## Phase 7: First Sale & Conversion Sprint (Weeks 13–14)
- **Playbooks**: [[playbook-launch-and-sales]], [[playbook-metrics-and-retros]]
- **Shiloh's Activities**: Follow up with waitlist + beta testers; fix top 3 onboarding friction points from PH feedback; run one $0–$25 pricing or offer experiment (e.g., $29 Founding Kit vs. $49 + Setup Guide).
- **Deliverables**: First paying US homeschool customer, Checkout-to-Setup friction audit, 1 onboarding improvement shipped.
- **Dad's Role**: Verify payment receipt, tax/bookkeeping entry, and customer delivery; deliver any paid parent onboarding calls.

## Phase 8: Grow & Systematize (Weeks 15+)
- **Playbooks**: [[playbook-content-founder-influencer]], [[playbook-metrics-and-retros]], [[playbook-next-product-reuse]]
- **Shiloh's Activities**: Publish monthly "Student-CEO Numbers" retro (real revenue, lessons, costs); decide whether to double down (add co-op tier), pivot, or spin up Venture #2 using [[playbook-next-product-reuse]].
- **Deliverables**: Quarterly Venture P&L & Retro, Decision Memo (`double-down` vs. `next-venture`).
- **Dad's Role**: Quarterly financial/tax check; approve next quarter's Venture scope.
