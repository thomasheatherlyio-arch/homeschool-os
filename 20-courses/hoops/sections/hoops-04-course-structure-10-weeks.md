## 4. Course Structure — 10 Weeks

Suggested pace: **Week 1 includes the baseline test** (Section 5 — not skipped), then **one focus per week**, with a short handle + form-shooting warm-up every practice. The tryout date is not set; this block is built to be **ready by late January 2027**. If the tryout falls later in February, Weeks 9–10 extend as polish and maintenance, not as a new crash course.

**Session guardrail:** 45–75 minutes on court, plus warm-up and cool-down. A session counts as course work **only** when a [[hoops-training-log]] entry is written the same day. The mentor asks, then cues, then explains — it does not write the log or invent scores.

Dates below are Monday–Sunday, starting the week of 2026-11-23. Adjust with Dad if Week 1 needs to start the following week; do not pretend a missed week was done.

### At-a-glance

| Week | Dates | Focus | On-court work | Deliverable / evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | 2026-11-23 – 11-29 | Baseline + stance, handle, footwork | Skill-placement test (Section 5); athletic stance, pivot, jab, pound dribbles | Completed baseline scoresheet + first log entry |
| **2** | 11-30 – 12-06 | Ball handling + passing | Both hands, change of direction, bounce/chest/outlet; no watching the ball on easy moves | Log: cone-dribble time + passing makes/attempts |
| **3** | 12-07 – 12-13 | Shooting form + free throws | One form cue at a time; close-in form shooting; 20 free throws recorded | Log: free-throw 20s and form notes (not just "I shot") |
| **4** | 12-14 – 12-20 | Finishing | Layups both hands, Mikan or timed layups, gather footwork, finish off one foot and two | Log: timed-layup count, both-hand notes |
| **5** | 12-21 – 12-27 | Holiday maintenance (lighter) | Short form-shooting and handle only; protect the rest day; no peak conditioning | Log: shorter session + rest day noted |
| **6** | 12-28 – 01-03 | Defense + mid-block retest | Stance, slides, closeouts, hand position; re-run a subset of the baseline | Mid-block scores next to Test 1 (same drills, same conditions if possible) |
| **7** | 01-04 – 01-10 | Rebounding + conditioning | Box-out, chase, two-hand chin, outlet; basketball sprints and lane agility (quality, not exhaustion) | Log: rebound/outlet work + timed lane agility or sprint |
| **8** | 01-11 – 01-17 | Basketball IQ | Spacing, pass/shot/drive reads; 2-on-2 or 3-on-3 if a group is available; otherwise film of a real game + written decisions | Film or live-play notes: 3 decisions he would take again or change |
| **9** | 01-18 – 01-24 | Tryout-style stations | Combine the week's skills in short stations; practice listening to a cue and changing the next rep | Log: station scores + one coachability note (what cue he applied) |
| **10** | 01-25 – 01-31 | Capstone retest + polish | Full baseline retest (Section 5 and Section 8); light sharpening, not a new program | Test 2 scoresheet + short reflection for Dad |

**Every week also includes:** a warm-up and cool-down, at least one rest day, and (on Kitchen days, when it fits) a planned pre- or post-practice snack or meal, logged as Kitchen evidence, not as a Hoops score.

**If energy, feet, or a schedule collision shows up:** shorten the session, keep the log, and tell Dad. Do not "make up" by stacking two hard days back to back.
