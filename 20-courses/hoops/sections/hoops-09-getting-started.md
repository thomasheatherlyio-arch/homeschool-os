## 9. Getting Started

This course is **draft-pending-approval** ([[20261123-hoops-course]]). Formal credit waits for Dad. The [[basketball|Hoops mentor]] seat is already active. Practice with [[jahari]] can continue either way.

A low-stakes first move, once Dad is fine with the test being run:

1. Confirm court access, who can time the baseline, and any foot or health limits with Dad (include the [[gym-course]] flat-feet work if it still applies).
2. Run the Section 5 baseline (Week 1) and paste results into the table and [[hoops-training-log]].
3. Tell the Hoops mentor what the scores were — in his own numbers — and pick the Week 2 focus. Do not skip the log.

No tryout date, team name, or position is required to start Week 1. Add those when they are known.
