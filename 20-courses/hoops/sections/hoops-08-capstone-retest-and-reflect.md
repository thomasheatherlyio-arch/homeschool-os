## 8. Capstone — Retest, Present, Reflect

Week 10 (or the week before the real tryout, if the date is earlier). No new system — run Test 2 under the same conditions as Test 1, then write.

**Deliverable (pending until Dad verifies):**

1. The completed baseline table in Section 5 (Test 1, mid-block, Test 2).
2. A short write-up in his own words (about one page): what improved, what did not, which cue he will take into the tryout, and how he practiced coachability and sportsmanship.
3. A brief show-and-tell to Dad (and Jahari if available): the scoresheet and one or two video clips if they exist. This is a presentation of evidence, not a performance for a grade the mentor assigns.

If the tryout is after January 31, keep a maintenance week (form shooting, handle, one live-play session if possible, rest day) and re-run only the drills that have slipped — don't pile on a new program the week of the tryout.
