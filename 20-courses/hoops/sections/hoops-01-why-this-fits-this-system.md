## 1. Why This Fits This System

Same logic [[gym-course|Doc 15]] and [[kitchen-course|Doc 14]] use for training and cooking, applied to basketball:

- **Every practice is a measurable experiment** — makes, attempts, and times either improve or they don't (Charter Principle 3). Nobody has to take Shiloh's word that he "worked on his shot."
- **A training log is executive-function practice** — show up, time-box, write the truth the same day, adjust next week. That is [[systems-self-command|Systems & Self-Command]] in gym clothes.
- **Shooting and stats are math** — makes ÷ attempts, trends over weeks, not vibes. That is [[mind-mastery|Mind & Mastery]] with a basketball.
- **Fuel is a Kitchen problem** — what to eat and drink before and after practice is [[kitchen-course]] work, not a guess from the internet.
- **Teams teach character** — listening to a coach, sprinting on a missed shot, treating teammates well. That is [[spirit-character|Spirit & Character]] and Good Citizenship lived out, the same family of habits [[civ-course]] asks him to practice.
- **It does not replace Jahari or The Gym.** Jahari remains the IRL basketball Knight. The Gym remains the science-of-training course. This track is the tryout-prep workshop that sits next to both.
