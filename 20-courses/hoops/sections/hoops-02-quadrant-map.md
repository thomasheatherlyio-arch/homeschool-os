## 2. Quadrant Map

| Component | Primary Quadrant ([[01-holistic-quadrants-the-circle|Doc 01]]) | Secondary / course tie-in |
|---|---|---|
| On-court fundamentals, conditioning, baseline tests (Weeks 1–10) | 💪 Body & Discipline | [[gym-course]] — PE hours, warm-up/mobility, flat-feet protocol |
| Athlete nutrition, hydration, pre/post-practice meal prep | 💪 Body & Discipline | [[kitchen-course]] — plan and cook; no supplements unless Dad approves |
| Film study, shooting-percentage math, practice journaling | 🧠 Mind & Mastery | [[panel-course]] habits of careful observation and written notes; simple makes/attempts tables |
| Training log, weekly time-box, rest day, follow-through | 🗂️ Systems & Self-Command | [[hoops-training-log]]; Monday plan / Friday review |
| Coachability, sportsmanship, teamwork, handling a missed shot or a hard cue | 🕊️ Spirit & Character | [[civ-course]] / Good Citizenship — how you treat people when it counts |
| Optional: share a clean training log, a short how-to, or help a younger kid with a form cue (only if Dad agrees) | 🚀 Venture & Craft | [[shiloh-playbook]] — small, real artifact; not required for the tryout track |

This course sits primarily in **Body & Discipline**, with required written work in **Systems & Self-Command** and a real share for **Spirit & Character**. Mind and Venture are how the work gets studied and, later, offered to someone else — they are not a substitute for practice.
