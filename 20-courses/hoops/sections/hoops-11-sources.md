## Sources

Public sources below are well-known governing or curriculum pages. Drill *formats* in Section 5 are standard coaching tests (timed layups, free throws out of 20, spot shooting, cone dribbles, lane agility, short sprints). **No invented averages, cutoffs, or "what a 14-year-old should run."** AAU does not publish one official tryout rubric; Section 7 describes typical categories, not a named program's scoresheet.

- [USA Basketball — Youth Basketball Guidelines](https://www.usab.com/play/youth-basketball-guidelines) (rest at least one day per week from organized basketball; delay single-sport specialization until age 14 or older; multi-sport participation supported)
- [USA Basketball Youth Development Guidebook (Player Development Curriculum)](https://cdn1.sportngin.com/attachments/document/0095/3630/200.30_16.3.24_USA_BASKETBALL_YOUTH_DEVELOPMENT_GUIDEBOOK.pdf) (skill categories and a mastery-based, not age-based, development model)

Film study in later weeks should use a game or clip Shiloh actually watches (his own film, a full public game, or a clip Jahari assigns) — not a made-up "scout report."
