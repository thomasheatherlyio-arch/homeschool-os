## 5. Skill Placement — Start Where He Actually Is

Current known starting point (as of 2026-11-23): Shiloh already trains with [[jahari]] (basketball is underway; see also [[gym-course]] §5). **Position, years of play, and a scored baseline are not in the vault yet.** Do not guess a level. Run this test in Week 1, then flex the weekly plan:

- **Handles and form already clean, times and percentages already solid** — compress Weeks 2–4 review and spend more time on finishing under pressure, defense, IQ, and scrimmage habits.
- **Form breaks down, or one hand / one side is clearly behind** — keep the weekly focus, and add a short daily form or weak-hand block (10–15 minutes), not a second full practice.
- **Little structured testing so far** — that is fine. Test 1 is the start line, not a grade.

**One rule that doesn't flex:** a test or practice without a same-day log does not count as course work.

### Baseline test (measurable drills)

Run **Test 1 in Week 1**, a **mid-block subset in Week 6**, and **Test 2 in Week 10**. Same warm-up, same court if possible, same shot counts and distances, someone else timing when they can. Record the court, who timed, and the date. **Leave targets blank until Test 1 exists** — do not invent "average 14-year-old" numbers.

Suggested order after a full warm-up (about 45–60 minutes for the whole test):

| Drill | How to run it | What to record |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Timed layups** | Alternating-hand layups (or a Mikan block, then a 60-second layup run starting under the basket) for **60 seconds**. Both hands count. | Makes in 60 seconds; note which hand missed more |
| **2. Free throws** | **20** free throws. One consistent routine. No "do-overs." | Makes / 20 and percentage (makes ÷ 20 × 100) |
| **3. Spot shooting** | **5 spots** (left corner, left wing, top, right wing, right corner). **5 shots** from each, mid-range or the range he actually uses in games — pick one distance and keep it for every test. | Makes / 5 per spot and 25-shot total |
| **4. Cone dribble** | A 4- or 5-cone zigzag or slalom, down and back. **Right hand**, then **left hand**. Best of two timed runs each; count losses of control. | Time (seconds) each hand; turnovers |
| **5. Lane agility** | Around the key: sprint, side-shuffle (defensive stance), and backpedal so each side of the lane is used. Same pattern every test. (This is the same *kind* of drill used at the NBA Draft Combine — use the shape, not professional times as a goal.) | Time (seconds); fouls on the pattern (hit a line, stood up out of stance) |
| **6. Sprint** | **Baseline to the opposite foul line** (a 3/4-court sprint) or, if the court has no clear foul line, **baseline to baseline**. Same distance every test. Best of two. | Time (seconds) |

If a partner is available, add (optional, not required for Test 1): wall or partner passing — chest and bounce — **30 seconds**, record completions. If not, skip it and say so on the sheet.

**Form notes (required, not scored as a time):** on shooting and finishing, write one sentence on balance, follow-through, or footwork. A pretty percentage with broken form is not the win.
