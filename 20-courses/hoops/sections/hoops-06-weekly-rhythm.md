## 6. Weekly Rhythm

Plugs into the normal [[04-student-dashboard|Doc 04]] rhythm, same as [[gym-course|Doc 15]] and [[civ-course]]:

- **Monday planning:** one of the week's missions can be a Hoops step — e.g., "Week 1: run the baseline test and log it," or "Week 3: 20 free throws recorded + form notes." Clock times stay draft until Shiloh and Dad lock them in [[shiloh-schedule]].
- **Practice days (about 3–4 per week):** 45–75 minutes on court, plus warm-up and cool-down. Same-day [[hoops-training-log]] entry required for the session to count. Optional video goes in `50-evidence/`.
- **Rest:** at least **one full day** with no organized basketball (USA Basketball youth guidance — see Sources). Week 5 is lighter on purpose.
- **Kitchen tie-in (when it fits the Kitchen plan):** one planned pre- or post-practice meal or snack, logged as Kitchen evidence.
- **Mind / film (once a week starting Week 8, optional earlier):** a short film clip or one recorded practice rep, with three written observations — not a full movie night.
- **"Hoops session":** mid-week planning or a same-day debrief with the [[basketball|Hoops mentor]]. Does not replace Friday review.
- **Friday review:** log finished pieces into the Evidence Inbox, tag quadrant (Section 2) and subject (Section 3), and check whether the rest day and the log actually happened. Pending until Dad verifies.

This track should not steal approved Khan minutes or blow up the school-day draft. If a conflict appears, shorten basketball, don't silently drop ELA or science.
