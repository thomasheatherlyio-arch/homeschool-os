## 3. Texas Curriculum Tie-In (per [[03-academic-tracker-tx-compliance|Doc 03]])

The proposal ([[20261123-hoops-course]]) asks Thomas to decide **how this course counts** — recommended: Health & PE hours **alongside** [[gym-course]], not a second standalone PE course, and not a replacement for The Gym. Until he designates it, nothing is counted as complete. This table is the intended mapping, not a completed credit.

| Subject | TX status ([[03-academic-tracker-tx-compliance|Doc 03]] §1) | How The Hoops covers it | Evidence type |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Health & Physical Education** | Standard practice | The on-court plan: fundamentals, conditioning, rest, and safe practice habits | [[hoops-training-log]], baseline/retest scoresheets, session notes |
| **Science** | Standard practice | Light tie to [[gym-course]] physiology already in that course (why warm-up, why rest days) — this course does not duplicate those units | Pointers in the log to Gym work when it applies |
| **Mathematics** | Required | Makes ÷ attempts, free-throw and spot-shooting percentages, time comparisons across tests | Scoresheets and a short percentage table in the log or journal |
| **Reading** | Required | Short film or skill notes from a named public source (Section 11), in his own words | Reading / film notes |
| **Spelling & Grammar** | Required | Session logs, Week 10 reflection, tryout-prep write-up | Log entries and the capstone reflection |
| **Good Citizenship** | Required | Coachability, sportsmanship, teamwork — how he takes a cue and treats teammates (Section 7) | Session takeaways; Jahari or Dad observation when available |
| **Executive Function** | Core to the plan ([[03-academic-tracker-tx-compliance|Doc 03]] §4) | Same-day logging, a rest day, a 10-week follow-through | [[hoops-training-log]], weekly missions, Friday review |

Log every right-hand-column item into [[03-academic-tracker-tx-compliance|Doc 03]]'s Evidence Log as it's produced — same discipline as The Kitchen, The Gym, The Panel, and The Civ. Hours and credit are Thomas's to assign.
