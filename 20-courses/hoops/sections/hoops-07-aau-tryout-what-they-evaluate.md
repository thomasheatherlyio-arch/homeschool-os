## 7. What AAU Tryouts Typically Evaluate

There is **no single official AAU scoring sheet**. Clubs and age-group events differ, and this vault does not invent a rubric or a cutoff score. What follows is the common pattern at youth travel / AAU-style and school tryouts, lined up with USA Basketball's player-development skill categories (ball handling and dribbling, footwork and body control, passing and receiving, rebounding, screening, shooting, team defense, team offense — see Sources).

Coaches usually watch **stations and live play**, not just one drill:

| Area | What they tend to watch |
|---|---|
| **Ball handling** | Control with both hands, head up on simple moves, poise when the drill speeds up |
| **Passing** | On-time, on-target, seeing a teammate — not only a fancy feed |
| **Finishing** | Layups with both hands, footwork, finishing through a little traffic without panicking |
| **Shooting** | Repeatable form, balance, free throws, catch-and-shoot from the spots he will actually get |
| **Defense** | Stance, slides, staying between the ball and the basket, effort on closeouts |
| **Rebounding / effort plays** | Boxing out, chasing, sprinting in transition, 50/50 balls |
| **Athleticism & conditioning** | Can he still move well late in the session? (This is why the sprint and lane-agility times exist — as *his* trend, not as a copied pro number.) |
| **Basketball IQ** | Spacing, knowing where to stand, a simple read (pass, shot, drive) without being told every time |
| **Coachability** | Faces the coach, starts in the right spot, changes the *next* rep after a cue |
| **Team behavior & composure** | Teammates, body language after a miss, no blaming, still engaged off the ball |

USA Basketball's youth guidelines also emphasize health over early burnout: rest, and not treating this block as a reason to drop every other activity. This course is a tryout-prep season, not a demand to specialize and ignore The Gym, school work, or other sports Dad already supports.

**What this means for Shiloh:** Weeks 1–7 build the skill stations; Weeks 8–10 practice looking like a teammate and a listener under tryout conditions. A clean log and a better Test 2 matter. So does the one-line coachability note in Weeks 9–10.

When the actual program, age division, and tryout date are known, add them here and adjust Week 9–10 — do not rewrite the fundamentals.
