# Doc 14 — The Kitchen: Culinary Studio, Menu Design & Food Systems Course

**Purpose:** Shiloh has chosen to spend his afternoons on chef/cooking skills — menu creation, researching where food actually comes from, and sourcing ingredients. This doc turns that choice into a real course: a structured but self-paced curriculum that treats cooking as a legitimate career/life-skill track (per Doc 10's exposure-ladder logic) *and* a vehicle for real Texas-curriculum subject credit (per [[03-academic-tracker-tx-compliance|Doc 03]]) — math, health, science, and more, taught through a kitchen instead of a workbook, per Thomas's direction that this count academically wherever it honestly can.
