## 10. Career & Interest-Exploration Tie-In

Per Doc 10, this only becomes a real Interest Ledger candidate once it's been tested, not just enjoyed in the abstract:

- **Rung 1 (informational interview):** a working chef, caterer, or restaurant owner — the Unit 5 sourcing trip is a natural place to also ask a vendor who they'd introduce Shiloh to.
- **Rung 2 (job shadow):** a kitchen shadow day, once a contact exists — same Headmaster sign-off process as any other outside placement ([[00-governing-charter-and-toc|Doc 00]] §4, Doc 10 §4).
- **Rung 4 (limited paid work, age 14–15):** food service is one of the specific categories Texas actually permits at this age (Doc 10 §2) — worth knowing now, relevant later, not an immediate next step.
- **[[13-the-work-ladder-home-job-and-first-job|Doc 13]] tie-in:** cooking real family meals can become a paid "family chef" line on the Work Ladder's Extras Menu ([[13-the-work-ladder-home-job-and-first-job|Doc 13]] §2.2) once the fundamentals are solid — a natural bridge from "course" to "real, valued household contribution."

Log any of this in the Exposure Log (Doc 10 §6) and Interest Ledger ([[07-north-star-life-path|Doc 07]] §6) as it happens.
