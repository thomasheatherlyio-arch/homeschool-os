## 6. Weekly Rhythm

This is an afternoon elective, so it plugs into the normal [[04-student-dashboard|Doc 04]] rhythm rather than replacing it:

- **Monday planning:** one of the week's 3–5 missions can be a Kitchen unit step — e.g., "research where coffee/beef/wheat actually comes from" or "cost out the appetizer course."
- **Afternoons:** actual kitchen time — cooking, research, sourcing trips. Capture evidence same-day per [[04-student-dashboard|Doc 04]]'s "capture as you go" habit (a photo of the dish, the written costing sheet, the research notes).
- **Friday review:** log finished pieces into the Evidence Inbox, tag them to the right quadrant (Section 2) and subject (Section 3), and note how the unit's actually going — this is also the natural check-in point for whether the pace needs to change.
