## 7. Small Bet & Project Contract

Per [[07-north-star-life-path|Doc 07]] §3, this starts as a small bet, not a locked-in semester commitment — and per [[04-student-dashboard|Doc 04]] §3, a project this size gets its own lightweight contract, modeled on [[00-governing-charter-and-toc|Doc 00]] §7.

**Suggested first small bet:** *"Run Units 1–3 (fundamentals, math, science/nutrition) over the next 3–4 weeks. At the end, log it in the Interest Ledger honestly: does this still feel worth continuing into the sourcing/menu/business units, or was this a shorter-lived interest?"*

A project contract should cover:

- **What Shiloh commits to:** showing up to scheduled kitchen afternoons, finishing each unit's deliverable rather than leaving dishes/research half-done, and treating knife/stove safety rules as non-negotiable.
- **What Thomas provides:** grocery budget for practice cooking and the sourcing trips, supervision appropriate to the skill level in Section 5, and time in the weekly plan.
- **What "done" looks like per unit:** the deliverable in the Section 4 table, actually finished and logged.
- **Review point:** end of Unit 3 (the small-bet checkpoint above), and again at each Friday/Quarterly review after that.
