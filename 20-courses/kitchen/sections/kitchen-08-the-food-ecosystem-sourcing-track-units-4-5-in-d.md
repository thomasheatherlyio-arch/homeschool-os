## 8. The Food Ecosystem & Sourcing Track (Units 4–5, in depth)

This is the piece Shiloh specifically asked for, so it's worth spelling out rather than leaving it as one table row:

**Research questions worth chasing in Unit 4** (pick 2–3 ingredients to trace end-to-end): Where is this actually grown/raised? How does it get from there to a Texas grocery store or market? What's the difference between how it reaches a big chain store versus a local farmers market? Who actually gets paid along that chain, and how much of the price is the ingredient itself versus transport, processing, and markup?

**Turning it into practice in Unit 5:** a real trip — a grocery store, a farmers market, and (if accessible) a local farm or CSA pickup — comparing the same or similar ingredients across all three: price, freshness, where it's actually from, and how far it traveled. This is also a natural informational-interview opportunity per Doc 10 §4 — a farmer or market vendor is a legitimate Rung 1 contact, with the same five-question script Doc 10 already uses, adapted to food ("How did you get into this, what does a normal week look like, what's the part nobody tells you about?").
