## 9. Capstone — Menu Night

**Required minimum:** Shiloh plans, sources, and cooks a full multi-course menu solo for the family — this is the "Build & Make" example already named in [[11-the-studio-experiential-learning-menu|Doc 11]] §4's activity menu, now given a full course behind it. Menu gets designed (Unit 6), costed (Unit 7), and themed around what he learned in Unit 8's cuisine research.

**Stretch goal (optional, real Venture & Craft swing):** turn it into an actual small pop-up — invite a few extra people (family friends), price it like Unit 7's costing exercise for real, and run the whole night like a one-time small food business. This is the kind of bigger bet [[07-north-star-life-path|Doc 07]] §3 describes — worth doing only if the required minimum went well and Shiloh wants to keep going.

Either version gets filmed if Shiloh wants it to double as Studio content (Section 11).
