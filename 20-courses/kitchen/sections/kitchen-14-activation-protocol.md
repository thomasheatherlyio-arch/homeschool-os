## 13. Activation Protocol

To start or continue work on this course in conversation:

> "COLT, let's start Unit 1 of The Kitchen course." / "COLT, help me research where [ingredient] actually comes from." / "COLT, help me cost out this menu." / "COLT, quiz me on food safety before I start cooking."

To connect it to the rest of the system:

> "Log this in the Evidence Inbox." / "Does this sourcing trip count as a Doc 10 exposure contact?" / "Add this to the Interest Ledger."
