## 5. Skill Placement — Start Where He Actually Is

Before Unit 1 locks in, run a quick, honest self-check with Shiloh (COLT can run this conversationally) rather than assuming a level:

- **New to real cooking** → start Unit 1 at full length, with Thomas supervising knife work and stove/oven use directly until comfort is demonstrated, not assumed.
- **Comfortable making simple meals solo already** → Unit 1 can compress to a safety-checklist review plus one demonstration dish, then move faster into Units 2–3.
- **Already cooks regularly and confidently** → compress Units 1–2, and let him run more of Units 4–5 (research, sourcing trip planning) independently, with Thomas checking the final plan rather than co-planning it.

This is a home-kitchen course under direct parental supervision, not paid employment — Doc 10 §2's age-based restrictions (open flame, deep fryers, etc.) govern *outside paid work*, not cooking at home with Thomas present. Worth knowing anyway, because it's exactly the kind of real-world detail that'll matter later if food service ever becomes an actual Rung 4 job (Section 10).
