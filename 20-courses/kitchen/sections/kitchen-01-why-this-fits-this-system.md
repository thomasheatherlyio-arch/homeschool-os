## 1. Why This Fits This System

[[03-academic-tracker-tx-compliance|Doc 03]] §6 already names the strategy this doc leans on hardest: *"Route content through his real interests where possible... motivation follows relevance."* Cooking is an unusually good vehicle for that, because almost nothing about it is single-subject:

- **Every recipe is a math problem** — scaling, ratios, unit conversion, and (once menu costing starts) real arithmetic with real financial stakes.
- **Every dish is a science experiment** — heat, chemistry, and biology are happening whether or not anyone names them, which makes this a natural home for [[11-the-studio-experiential-learning-menu|Doc 11]] §2.5-style edutainment ("why does bread actually rise?").
- **Every ingredient has a supply chain** — sourcing research is genuine social-studies/civics material ([[03-academic-tracker-tx-compliance|Doc 03]]'s "good citizenship" subject), not a stretch.
- **A planned menu is executive-function practice in disguise** — mise en place (literally "everything in its place") is [[01-holistic-quadrants-the-circle|Doc 01]]'s Systems & Self-Command quadrant, wearing an apron.
- **A finished, served meal is real evidence** — per [[00-governing-charter-and-toc|Doc 00]] Principle 3, nobody has to take Shiloh's word for it that he learned something; a plated menu, a costing sheet, and a family's dinner are the paper trail.

This also slots directly into Doc 10's exposure-ladder logic: food service is one of the specific occupation categories Texas actually permits for 14–15-year-olds (Doc 10 §2), which means "chef" isn't just a fun unit — it's a real, testable career candidate for the Interest Ledger ([[07-north-star-life-path|Doc 07]] §3), the same way HVAC or welding is.
