## 3. Texas Curriculum Tie-In (per [[03-academic-tracker-tx-compliance|Doc 03]])

This is the part built specifically to satisfy [[03-academic-tracker-tx-compliance|Doc 03]]'s requirements — every required subject gets a real, honest home in this course, not a token mention.

| Subject | TX status ([[03-academic-tracker-tx-compliance|Doc 03]] §1) | How The Kitchen covers it | Evidence type |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mathematics** | Required | Recipe scaling and unit conversion (weights, volumes, ounces↔grams, cups↔liters), ratios, yield percentages, food costing, grocery budgeting | Conversion worksheets, costing sheets, annotated receipts |
| **Reading** | Required | Following real recipes/cookbooks, reading food-science and food-ecosystem source material, researching cuisines | Reading log, source notes, annotated recipes |
| **Spelling & Grammar** | Required | Written menus, recipe write-ups in his own words, the food-ecosystem research report (Unit 4) | Draft menus, written reports |
| **Good Citizenship** | Required | Food-ecosystem research (supply chains, local agriculture, food access), supporting local farmers/producers directly through sourcing | Research report, farmers-market/sourcing-trip reflection |
| **Science** | Standard practice | Food science (Maillard reaction, emulsification, fermentation, yeast activity), food safety & microbiology (safe temps, cross-contamination) | Experiment write-ups (e.g., a controlled bread-rise test), a food-safety quiz/checklist he builds himself |
| **Health & PE** | Standard practice | Nutrition science, reading nutrition labels, macro/micronutrients, designing a genuinely balanced menu | Nutrition breakdown of a designed menu |
| **History / Social Studies** | Standard practice | Culinary history and world-cuisine unit (Section on Cuisine & Culture below) | Research notes or a short presentation |
| **Executive Function** | Core to the plan ([[03-academic-tracker-tx-compliance|Doc 03]] §4) | Planning a sourcing trip, running mise en place, executing a multi-course dinner on schedule | Weekly mission logs, Friday review notes |

[[03-academic-tracker-tx-compliance|Doc 03]]'s evidence log (Section 5 there) should get an entry every time one of the right-hand column items gets produced — this course is meant to generate real 7th/8th-grade-equivalent evidence, not just be a fun elective sitting outside the record.
