## 13a. Addendum — The First Small Bet: "Opening Night" (6-Week Restaurant Course)

*Added 2026-08-24, per Thomas.* The Section 7 small bet became a concrete, scoped project rather than staying abstract: Shiloh's whole course got reframed as **opening his own restaurant**, start to finish, over 6 weeks. This addendum records the shape of that concrete run so future sessions have it, even though the actual fillable workbook lives outside the project (delivered to Thomas as a Word document built to open cleanly in Google Docs — he uploads it to Drive and it converts automatically — which Shiloh fills in twice a week).

**Structure:** 6 weeks, 2 work sessions/week (12 sessions total), 3 videos due every 2 weeks, one real capstone dinner.

| Week | Focus | Ties to Section 4 units |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Find Your Concept — brainstorm, competitive research | Menu design groundwork |
| 2 | Build the Menu & Learn the Rules — draft menu v1, TX food-handler/health-inspection/licensing research | Units 6, 1 |
| 3 | Where Your Food Actually Comes From — sourcing plan, recipe test #1 | Units 4–5 |
| 4 | Learning From a Real Founder — interview prep + the interview itself | Doc 10 exposure ladder |
| 5 | Price It Like a Real Business — menu v2, food costing & pricing | Units 2, 7 |
| 6 | Open Your Doors — brand/experience, run-of-show, capstone | Units 6–7, capstone (Section 9) |

**The three videos:** Video 1 (end of Wk 2) — a 2–3 min pitch of the restaurant concept. Video 2 (end of Wk 4) — a recorded informational interview (Doc 10 §4 process) with **Naomi Seifter, founder of Picnik** (Austin; started 2013 from a food trailer, built it into a full restaurant and retail brand, and — as of July 2026 — is relaunching Picnik into a new concept, which makes her mid-decision on exactly the kind of choices Shiloh is practicing, not just recounting an old story). Video 3 (end of Wk 6) — the capstone Opening Night dinner, cooked and served to the family on camera, plus a closing reflection.

**Also folded in:** the Texas food-handler-certificate fact from research earlier in this project (no state minimum age — Shiloh could realistically earn a real certificate during Week 2) became a stretch-goal checkbox rather than just a fact.

**Closes the loop back to [[07-north-star-life-path|Doc 07]]:** the workbook's final page is a Interest Ledger-style reflection — confirmed / ruled out / still unclear — so this small bet actually resolves one way or another rather than fading out.
