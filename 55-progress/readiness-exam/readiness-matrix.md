## Bank strands

These strands match the filled draft banks (Texas Grade 8 TEKS / 9th-grade-entry readiness). Keys and rubrics live only in `keys/`.

- **Reading (12):** two short original passages ("The Second Timer," literary; "Why Ranch Windmills Still Matter," informational). Vocabulary in context (8.2); key ideas and inference (8.5F, 8.5G); summary (8.6D); literary elements (8.7); informational structure and author's craft (8.8D, 8.9); text evidence (8.6C).
- **Writing (12):** writing process — plan, develop, revise (8.10A–C); genres — literary, informational, argumentative, correspondence (8.11A–D). Longer prompts (W10–W12) use the Organization / Development / Conventions rubric in [[key-writing]].
- **Spelling & Grammar (12):** conventions 8.10D(i–vii) — complete sentences, agreement, pronouns, modifiers, prepositional phrases, commas and quotation marks — plus commonly confused words and spelling in context (8.2 / 8.10D).
- **Mathematics (15):** Grade 8 strands in [[bank-math]], with extra items on rational / real numbers (8.2B–D), bivariate statistics and trend (8.5C–D, 8.11A–B), transformations and dilations (8.10, 8.3C), 3D volume and surface area (8.7A–B), and pre-Algebra 1 (8.8C, 8.9A / A.5A). Also covered: scientific notation (8.2C), slope and linear relationships (8.4C, 8.5I), functions (8.5G), and the Pythagorean theorem (8.7C).
- **Good Citizenship (12):** constitutional principles (8.15D); Bill of Rights and later amendments (8.16, 8.19B); unalienable rights (8.19A); responsible citizenship (8.19C); Federalists and Anti-Federalists (8.17); civic participation and points of view (8.21, 8.22). Civic knowledge and judgment, not a character test.
- **Science (12):** matter and chemical vs. physical change (8.6); force and motion / Newton's laws (8.7); waves (8.8); the universe and the H-R diagram (8.9); weather (8.10); climate and the carbon cycle (8.11); ecosystems (8.12); cells, heredity, and adaptations (8.13).
- **History / Social Studies (12):** colonial America (8.2, 8.3); American Revolution (8.4); the Constitution and the early republic (8.5); westward expansion and sectionalism (8.6); Civil War (8.8); Reconstruction (8.9).
- **Health & PE (12):** fitness principles and training (PE 8.8); reading a simple activity log (8.9); everyday food and water habits for activity (8.10); safety in movement and play (8.11); social-emotional skills in groups (8.12–8.15); lifetime wellness habits (8.16). Self-report and scenario items only; no medical, disease, medication, diagnosis, or reproductive-health questions.
- **Executive function (12):** no specific Grade 8 TEKS course; 9th-grade-entry study skills and planning (some items also align to ELAR 8.10A). Starting a task, planning a short block, estimating time, organizing materials, handling distraction, shifting after a setback, working memory for multi-step directions, and leaving a next step. Self-report and scenario items; not a character test or a medical screening.
