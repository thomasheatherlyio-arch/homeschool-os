# Bank — History

**Status: draft** (pending Dad's approval of [[20261122-readiness-examiner]]). **Items: 12.** Answers live only in [[key-history]]. Do not open the key during a sitting.

**Strands:** colonial America and reasons for English colonization (8.2); American Revolution causes and key steps (8.4); the Constitution and the early republic in historical context (8.5); westward expansion and sectionalism (8.6); Civil War causes, course, and effects (8.8); Reconstruction (8.9). Civic details that belong mainly in Good Citizenship (Bill of Rights lists, everyday civic scenarios) are not the focus here.

Write short constructed answers in complete sentences unless the item is multiple choice.

### H1 (easy, constructed response) — TEKS 8.2B
Give two different reasons English settlers came to North America in the 1600s (for example: economic, religious, or political). Name a colony or region that fits one of those reasons.

### H2 (easy, multiple choice) — TEKS 8.4A / 8.4B
Which of the following was a major colonial grievance that helped lead to the American Revolution?
- A. Britain refused to let any colonists farm
- B. Colonists were required to join the French army
- C. Taxation without colonial representation in Parliament, along with other limits after the French and Indian War
- D. The Declaration of Independence had already been repealed

### H3 (medium, constructed response) — TEKS 8.4A, 8.4C
How did the outcome of the French and Indian War help set up later conflict between Britain and the colonies? Include at least one specific postwar British action or policy in your answer.

### H4 (medium, constructed response) — TEKS 8.4C / 8.4B
Why was the Declaration of Independence written? In two or three sentences, explain its purpose in 1776 and name one idea it used to justify breaking from Britain.

### H5 (medium, constructed response) — TEKS 8.5A / 8.5B
The first government under the Articles of Confederation had serious weaknesses. Name two of those weaknesses, and explain how the Constitution was meant to address one of them.

### H6 (medium, constructed response) — TEKS 8.6B / 8.6C
Name one major territorial change or acquisition in the early 1800s (for example, the Louisiana Purchase). What problem or debate did westward growth make harder for the United States to avoid?

### H7 (medium, multiple choice) — TEKS 8.8A / 8.7C
Which statement best describes a central cause of the Civil War?
- A. A sudden shortage of cotton in the North and nothing else
- B. Sectional conflict over slavery, its expansion into new territories, and related questions of state and federal power
- C. The United States joining a European war in 1860
- D. The end of Reconstruction in the 1860s, which happened before the war began

### H8 (hard, constructed response) — TEKS 8.8B / 8.8C
Choose **one** of the following and explain its importance to the course or outcome of the Civil War in several sentences: the Emancipation Proclamation; the Battle of Gettysburg or Vicksburg (1863); or the Union's advantages in industry and railroads. Use at least one accurate historical detail.

### H9 (hard, constructed response) — TEKS 8.9A / 8.9C
What was one goal of Reconstruction after the Civil War? Then explain one way Reconstruction changed life for formerly enslaved people **and** one limit or setback (for example, later restrictions or the end of federal protection). Stay factual; do not invent dates you do not know.

### H10 (medium, constructed response) — TEKS 8.1A / 8.5C
Place these four events in chronological order, then write one sentence about why the **second** event in your list matters to the event that follows it:
- Ratification of the U.S. Constitution
- The Civil War
- The Declaration of Independence
- The Louisiana Purchase

### H11 (medium, constructed response) — TEKS 8.3A / 8.2A
How did geography or environment shape early colonial life in **one** region (New England, the Middle Colonies, or the South)? Give two concrete details (work, settlement, or resources).

### H12 (easy, constructed response) — TEKS 8.4C / 8.22A
George Washington is often mentioned in both the Revolution and the early presidency. Give one accurate contribution from the war years and one from the presidency (for example, a precedent, a policy, or a decision to leave office).
