**Strands:** complete sentences and fragments (8.10D(i)); verbs and subject-verb agreement (8.10D(ii)); pronouns (8.10D(iii)); adjectives, adverbs, and conjunctive adverbs (8.10D(iv)); prepositions and prepositional phrases (8.10D(v)); commas, semicolons, colons, italics, underlining, quotation marks (8.10D(vi)–(vii)); commonly confused words and spelling in context (8.2 / 8.10D).

Write the corrected sentence, the chosen letter, or a short explanation as the item asks. Do not change meaning unless the item says to revise.

### G2 (easy, multiple choice) — TEKS 8.10D(ii)

- A. The windmill and the electric pump is both ways to move water.
- B. The gears change spinning motion into an up-and-down stroke.
- C. A well-maintained mill run for decades.
- D. Bolts and wooden vanes loosens over time.

### G5 (easy, constructed response) — TEKS 8.10D(v)

### G8 (medium, constructed response) — TEKS 8.10D(vii) / commonly confused words
Choose the correct word in each pair and write the finished sentence.
1. The ranch kept (its / it's) windmill as backup during a power outage.
2. (There / Their / They're) planning to check the bolts on Tuesday.
3. I would (of / have) started the second batch sooner if I had written the note first.
4. The two pumps work (to / too / two) differently to treat as the same tool.
