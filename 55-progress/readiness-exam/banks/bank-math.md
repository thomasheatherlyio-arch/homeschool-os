# Bank — Math

**Status: draft** (pending Dad's approval of [[20261122-readiness-examiner]]). **Items: 15.** Work and answers live only in [[key-math]]. Do not open the key during a sitting.

**Strands (Grade 8, with extra items starred):** real numbers and scientific notation (8.2); slope and linear relationships (8.4, 8.5I); functions (8.5G); *rational / real number sense (8.2B–D); *bivariate statistics and trend (8.5C–D, 8.11A–B); equations with variables on both sides and *pre-Algebra 1 (8.8C, 8.9A / A.5A); Pythagorean theorem (8.7C); *transformations and dilations (8.10, 8.3C); *3D volume and surface area (8.7A–B).

Show enough work that a scorer can follow your reasoning. You may leave answers in terms of π when the item allows it. A sitting may use a subset if 15–25 minutes will not cover all 15; prefer a mix that still hits one starred strand.

### M1 (easy, constructed response) — TEKS 8.2C
1. Write \(6.4 \times 10^{4}\) in standard form.
2. Write \(0.00057\) in scientific notation.

### M2 (easy, constructed response) — TEKS 8.2D
Order these numbers from least to greatest: \(-3.2\), \(\frac{1}{2}\), \(-\sqrt{9}\), \(2.1\), \(\sqrt{5}\). Briefly explain how you placed \(\sqrt{5}\).

### M3 (medium, constructed response) — TEKS 8.2B
Between which two consecutive integers does \(\sqrt{50}\) lie? How do you know? Is \(\sqrt{50}\) rational or irrational? Approximate \(\sqrt{50}\) to the nearest tenth and show the squares you used.

### M4 (medium, constructed response) — TEKS 8.4C
A line passes through the points \((1, 3)\) and \((5, 11)\).
1. Find the slope.
2. Find the y-intercept.
3. Write the equation of the line in slope-intercept form.

### M5 (medium, constructed response) — TEKS 8.5I, 8.5G
A gym charges a \$25 start fee and \$8 per class. Let \(C\) be the total cost in dollars after \(n\) classes.
1. Write an equation for \(C\) in terms of \(n\).
2. In that equation, what does the number 8 represent in the situation?
3. If this relationship is a function, what is the input and what is the output?

### M6 (medium, constructed response) — TEKS 8.7C
A right triangle has legs of 9 feet and 12 feet. Find the length of the hypotenuse. Write the Pythagorean theorem with your numbers, then simplify.

### M7 (medium, constructed response) — TEKS 8.8C
Solve \(3x - 4 = 5x + 10\). Show each step. Check your solution in the original equation.

### M8 (hard, constructed response) — TEKS 8.8C; Algebra I A.5A (look-ahead)
Company A charges \$40 plus \$6 per hour. Company B charges \$16 plus \$10 per hour. After how many hours is the cost the same? What is that cost? Write an equation, solve it, and check both companies at that time.

### M9 (medium, constructed response) — TEKS 8.5C, 8.5D
Six students reported hours practiced and typing speed (words per minute):

| Hours practiced | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Speed (wpm) | 16 | 24 | 28 | 28 | 36 | 48 |

1. Is the association positive, negative, or neither? How can you tell from the table?
2. Would a linear model be a reasonable (even if not perfect) way to describe the trend? Why or why not?
3. Using the trend, about what speed would you expect near 3.5 hours? Give a range or estimate and explain it from the table — do not invent a precise value the table does not support.

### M10 (hard, constructed response) — TEKS 8.11A, 8.11B
Use the table from M9.
1. A student claims, "Every extra hour of practice adds exactly 10 wpm." Is that claim supported? Use at least two pairs of points.
2. Find the mean of the six speeds.
3. Find the mean absolute deviation (MAD) of the six speeds. What does that MAD tell you about how spread out the speeds are?

### M11 (medium, constructed response) — TEKS 8.10C
Point \(A\) is at \((3, -2)\).
1. Reflect \(A\) across the y-axis. Call the image \(A'\). What are the coordinates of \(A'\)?
2. Then translate \(A'\) 4 units up. Call the final image \(A''\). What are the coordinates of \(A''\)?

### M12 (hard, constructed response) — TEKS 8.3C, 8.10A, 8.10D
Triangle \(ABC\) is dilated by a scale factor of 2 with the center at the origin. Vertex \(A\) is at \((3, 1)\).
1. What are the coordinates of \(A'\)?
2. If a side of \(\triangle ABC\) has length 5 cm, what is the corresponding side length of \(\triangle A'B'C'\)?
3. If the area of \(\triangle ABC\) is 10 cm\(^{2}\), what is the area of \(\triangle A'B'C'\)? Explain the scale factor you use for area.
4. Does this dilation preserve orientation (does the triangle "flip")? Explain briefly.

### M13 (medium, constructed response) — TEKS 8.7A
A cylinder has radius 3 cm and height 10 cm.
1. Write the volume in terms of \(\pi\), including units.
2. Then give a decimal approximation using \(\pi \approx 3.14\).

### M14 (medium, constructed response) — TEKS 8.7B
A rectangular prism is 8 inches long, 5 inches wide, and 3 inches high. Find the **total** surface area. Show the three face areas you use. Include units.

### M15 (hard, constructed response) — TEKS 8.9A; Algebra I A.5A (look-ahead)
The lines \(y = 2x + 1\) and \(y = -x + 7\) intersect at one point. Find that point by solving the system. Check your point in both equations.
