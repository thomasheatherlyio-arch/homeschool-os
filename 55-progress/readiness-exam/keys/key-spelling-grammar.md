# Key — Spelling & Grammar (COLT / Dad only)

Shiloh should not open this file before or during a sitting. COLT never reads a key aloud during a test.

**Scoring:** Multiple choice (G2, G7) = 1 or 0. Constructed response = 2 complete and accurate, 1 partial, 0 blank or mostly wrong. **Total: 22 points.**

### G1
1. Fragment. Possible rewrite: "After the timer rang, Maya stopped." (any complete sentence that keeps the idea).
2. Complete sentence.
3. Fragment. Possible rewrite: "The sauce broke because the oil was added too quickly."

### G2 — **Correct: B.** A should be *are*; C should be *can run* / *runs*; D should be *loosen*.

### G3
Example: "Yesterday the cattle drank from the trough, and the mill lifted water all afternoon." Agreement and past tense must both be fixed.

### G4
A clear rewrite names who said what and what was left. Example: "When Jordan gave Riley the lab notes, Riley said she would copy them before class, but she left the notes on the table." Accept he/she/they if each pronoun has a stated antecedent and *it* is not used for plural notes.

### G5
Phrases: *Before sunrise* (when); *in the north pasture* (where / which pump); *into the tank* (where). All three needed for a 2.

### G6
"After the first batch failed, Maya turned the heat down, measured the oil more slowly, and started the timer again." Introductory clause comma + commas in a series of three verbs. No comma after *Maya*.

### G7 — **Correct: A.** Comma before the quotation and the period inside the closing quotation marks (American convention used in this bank).

### G8
1. its
2. They're
3. have (not *of*)
4. too
All four correct = 2; two or three = 1; fewer = 0.

### G9
maintained; underground; cattle; actually; receives. All five = 2; three or four = 1; fewer = 0.

### G10
A sound rewrite, for example: "The Constitution limits government, and it also protects individual rights. Later amendments extended some of those protections. The Bill of Rights was added early, and people still study those first ten amendments today." (Or a semicolon / period in place of the first splice; the fragment must be attached or completed.) 2 if all three problems are fixed without new fragments; 1 if one problem remains.

### G11
The runner must be the subject. Example: "Running across the pasture, we finally saw the trough." or "As we ran across the pasture, the trough was finally in sight."

### G12
Examples: "The first sauce broke; however, Maya did not quit, and she turned the heat down and tried again." or "The first sauce broke; Maya did not quit, so she turned the heat down and tried again." 2 if it is one grammatical sentence using a semicolon and/or conjunctive adverb, with no comma splice. 1 if ideas are combined but punctuation is weak.
