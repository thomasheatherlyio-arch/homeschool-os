# Key — Math (COLT / Dad only)

Shiloh should not open this file before or during a sitting. COLT never reads a key aloud during a test.

**Scoring:** Each item 0–2 (2 = complete and correct with required work; 1 = right idea with a slip, or missing a part; 0 = blank or conceptually wrong). Checks requested in M7, M8, and M15 are part of a complete score. **Total: 30 points.** Do not convert a sitting total into an official Doc 09a math level.

### M2
Least to greatest: \(-3.2\), \(-\sqrt{9}\), \(\frac{1}{2}\), \(2.1\), \(\sqrt{5}\).
Because \(-\sqrt{9} = -3\), and \(\sqrt{5}\) is a little more than \(2.2\) (since \(2.2^{2} = 4.84\) and \(2.3^{2} = 5.29\)), so \(\sqrt{5} > 2.1\). 1 if the order is right but the \(\sqrt{5}\) explanation is missing, or if \(-3.2\) and \(-3\) are swapped with otherwise correct order.

### M3
\(\sqrt{50}\) lies between 7 and 8 because \(7^{2} = 49\) and \(8^{2} = 64\). It is irrational (50 is not a perfect square; \(\sqrt{50}\) cannot be written as a ratio of integers). Nearest tenth: \(7.1\), because \(7.1^{2} = 50.41\) and \(7.0^{2} = 49\), and 50 is closer to 50.41 than to 49 (or compare 50.41 vs. midpoints). Accept a clear comparison of squares. 1 if the integers and irrational classification are right but the tenth is wrong or unjustified.

### M6

### M7
\(3x - 4 = 5x + 10\) → \(-4 - 10 = 5x - 3x\) → \(-14 = 2x\) → \(x = -7\).
Check: \(3(-7) - 4 = -25\) and \(5(-7) + 10 = -25\). 1 if the solution is right but there is no check, or a sign error is recovered in the check.

### M8
\(40 + 6h = 16 + 10h\) → \(40 - 16 = 10h - 6h\) → \(24 = 4h\) → \(h = 6\) hours. Cost: \(40 + 6(6) = 76\) dollars (Company B: \(16 + 10(6) = 76\)). 1 if hours are right but cost is missing or unchecked.

### M9
1. Positive: as hours increase, speeds generally increase (16 up to 48), even with a flat stretch at 3–4 hours.
2. A linear model is a reasonable rough description of the upward trend, but it is not perfect (same speed at 3 and 4 hours; a larger jump from 5 to 6). Either "reasonable but imperfect" or "only roughly" is acceptable if justified from the table.
3. Near 3.5 hours the table shows 28 wpm at both 3 and 4 hours, so an estimate around 28 wpm (or a narrow range such as mid-20s to low 30s centered on 28) is supported. Do not reward a made-up exact value such as 31.7 unless it is clearly labeled as a rough interpolation and stays near 28.

### M10
1. The claim is **not** supported. Counterexamples: from 1 to 2 hours the change is \(+8\), not \(+10\); from 3 to 4 hours the change is \(0\); from 5 to 6 hours the change is \(+12\). At least two pairs required for a 2.
2. Mean speed: \(\frac{16+24+28+28+36+48}{6} = \frac{180}{6} = 30\) wpm.
3. Absolute deviations from 30: \(14, 6, 2, 2, 6, 18\). MAD \(= \frac{48}{6} = 8\) wpm. The speeds typically sit about 8 wpm away from the mean (they are moderately spread, not all near 30). 1 if the mean is right but MAD is missing or mis-averaged.

### M14

### M15
Set equal: \(2x + 1 = -x + 7\) → \(3x = 6\) → \(x = 2\), \(y = 2(2) + 1 = 5\). Point \((2, 5)\).
Check: \(2(2) + 1 = 5\); \(-2 + 7 = 5\). 1 if the point is right with no check, or a small algebra slip is caught by a honest check.
