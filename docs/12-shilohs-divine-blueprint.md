Emotional security comes from having room to move, not from certainty.

Virgo risings tend to be quietly helpful, self-critical, health/body-aware, and more reliable in execution than their inner restlessness would suggest — a useful counterbalance worth naming, since it means the "system-building" muscle Doc 00/01 is trying to train isn't fighting his whole chart, just his Sun and Moon.

## 2. Vedic (Sidereal / Jyotish) Astrology

Same birth data, calculated with the Lahiri ayanamsa (the standard sidereal correction used in most Jyotish software), which shifts every placement back roughly 24° from the Western tropical positions above.

**Surya (Sun) Rashi: Pisces, 26.4°** — in Vedic astrology the Sun sign carries less weight than the Moon sign; Pisces Sun here suggests a softer, more intuitive/compassionate layer underneath the (Western) Aries drive.

**Chandra (Moon) Rashi: Scorpio, 9.6°** — in Vedic astrology the Moon sign is considered the single most important placement, since it governs the mind (*manas*) day-to-day. Scorpio Moon is intense, private, and deeply loyal once trust is earned — a notably different emotional read than the Western Sagittarius Moon above (open, exploratory), and both are worth sitting with rather than picking one as "true." A useful frame: Sagittarius Moon (Western) may describe how he *processes* feelings outwardly — talking, humor, motion — while Scorpio Moon (Vedic) may describe what's happening underneath, at a deeper and more private layer.

**Janma Nakshatra (birth star): Anuradha, pada 2** — the lunar mansion the Moon occupies, considered central to personality in Jyotish. Anuradha ("Star of Devotion," ruled by Saturn, symbolized by the lotus) is associated with emotional depth, loyalty, patience, and disciplined success that tends to arrive later rather than early — natives often see their real advancement after their late 20s/30s rather than in youth. Career strengths commonly associated with Anuradha: management, diplomacy, law, research, healing professions, and international/cross-border work.

**Lagna (Ascendant): Leo, 8.3°** — the Vedic rising sign, considered as important as the Sun sign in Western astrology. Leo Lagna carries natural leadership presence, confidence, and a wish to be seen doing meaningful things well — notably reinforcing the Western Mars-in-Virgo/Aries-Sun drive to *lead and act*, from a different angle.

## 3. Nine Star Ki (Kigaku)

A Japanese/Chinese-derived system that assigns a **Main (Principal) Star** from birth year, using the East Asian solar calendar (new year begins ~Feb 4, not Jan 1 — irrelevant here since April is well past that cutoff).

**Main Star: 6 — Six White Metal (Qian / Heaven trigram)**

Calculated and cross-checked two independent ways: (a) the standard formula — sum the digits of the birth year to a single digit, subtract from 11 (2012 → 2+0+1+2=5 → 11−5=6) — and (b) the published year-to-number reference cycle, which independently lands on 6 for 2012. Both agree.

**What Six Metal tends to mean:** natural anchor for a group — brings solidarity, structure, and an ethical backbone to whatever team or project he's part of. Practical problem-solver, drawn toward mechanical/structural fields and precise, hands-on craft (a notable echo of the Virgo Rising / Mars-in-Virgo pattern above). Prefers proven methods over untested ones; communicates through action and consistency more than words. Can have real intuition, sometimes verging on a "gut sense" people trust. The shadow side: rigidity, being "hard-headed," or turning self-critical/perfectionist under stress — worth watching for, since it rhymes with the Virgo Rising perfectionism theme too.

**Limitation to flag honestly:** Nine Star Ki traditionally also derives a *Character* (spirit) number from birth month and an *Energy* number from birth day, using a specialized Ki calendar (not simple digit math) — those weren't calculated here and would need a dedicated Nine Star Ki calculator run against the exact birth data to add real accuracy. The Main Star above is the reliable, most commonly cited number.

## 4. Human Design

**Type: Manifest Generator** (already established).

Manifest Generators are the "multi-hyphenate" type of Human Design — energized, passionate, capable of pursuing several interests at once, and (when aligned) able to hone a wide range of skills with a kind of contagious momentum. This lines up notably well with Doc 01's Circle (five active quadrants rather than one narrow track) and Doc 07's Interest Ledger/small-bets approach — the design is genuinely built for breadth before narrowing, not in spite of it.

**Strategy: Respond.** Manifest Generators aren't designed to sit and plan a move from scratch (that's a Manifestor or Projector pattern) — they're designed to respond to what's actually in front of them: an opportunity, a question, a "want to try this?" The sacral "yes" is where the real signal lives, not the mental pros-and-cons list. Practically, this suggests Doc 07's small-bets framework (cheap, fast, real-world experiments to respond to) fits his actual design better than long abstract planning sessions.

**Signature feeling: Satisfaction. Not-self theme: Frustration.** When he's doing things that genuinely light him up, satisfaction is the tell that he's aligned. Frustration — especially the feeling of being slowed down, or doing something out of obligation rather than real interest — is the signal that he's overridden his own "yes/no" to meet someone else's expectation (a teacher's, a parent's, even his own perfectionist streak). Doc 00's "systems over willpower" principle and Doc 07's Fork Log both benefit from treating frustration as diagnostic information, not just a mood to push through.

**What's not yet nailed down:** Type is the headline of a Human Design chart, but the full picture — Profile (e.g. 1/3, 6/2), Inner Authority (which determines *how* he should actually make decisions — sacral, emotional, splenic, etc.), defined/undefined Centers, and the specific Gates/Channels activated — all require running the exact birth data through a dedicated Human Design calculator (Swiss Ephemeris-based, same underlying math as the astrology above, but mapped onto Human Design's 64-gate mandala rather than the zodiac signs). That calculation wasn't attempted manually here to avoid guessing at gate numbers and stating something inaccurate in a doc meant to be trusted. **Recommended next step:** run the birth data (4/9/2012, 4:09 PM, Austin TX) through a free calculator like mybodygraph.com or myhumandesign.com — takes under a minute — and this doc can be updated with the full chart.

## 6. Other Personality & Persona-Mapping Options Worth Running

The systems above are archetypal/esoteric — genuinely useful for language, self-reflection, and pattern-spotting, but not evidence in the Doc 00 §3 sense ("evidence over assertion"). The tools below are more empirically grounded and pair well with Doc 07's North Star work, especially the Five Audits and Interest Ledger. None require birth data — just Shiloh's own answers.

**Holland Code / RIASEC** — the single most directly *career-relevant* tool on this list. Sorts interests into six types (Realistic, Investigative, Artistic, Social, Enterprising, Conventional) and maps the resulting 2-3 letter code directly onto real O*NET job titles and college majors. Free teen-specific version exists and takes 10-15 minutes. This is probably the highest-value next assessment to run, given the whole project is oriented around career/interest exploration.

**CliftonStrengths (Gallup) — StrengthsExplorer for ages 10-14** — identifies natural talent themes rather than weaknesses to fix, which fits Doc 00's "systems over willpower" philosophy well. Shiloh is right at the edge of the age band (14) between StrengthsExplorer (10-14) and the full CliftonStrengths for Students (15+) — either is reasonable now, and running the full version again at 15 for comparison could itself be an interesting data point.

**VIA Character Strengths — VIA-Youth Survey (ages 13-17)** — free, research-validated, measures character strengths (curiosity, perseverance, leadership, kindness, etc.) rather than career interest specifically. Fits well as a Spirit & Character quadrant (Doc 01) tool alongside Jahari's mentorship, not just a career tool.

**Enneagram** — nine core motivation-based types, more focused on *why* someone does what they do (core fears/desires) than *what* they're good at. Genuinely popular with teens and there's teen-specific material (Elizabeth Wagele's *The Enneagram for Teens*, Beth McCord's teen-focused content) — worth trying as a conversation-starter with Compass, but worth being honest that it's self-report and interpretive, similar in spirit to the astrology/Human Design systems above rather than a hard science.

**MBTI** — the most famous personality framework, but worth a caveat: it has real test-retest reliability critiques (people often get a different type on retake), and career counselors increasingly favor Holland Code or CliftonStrengths for actual career-matching. Fine as one more data point and a shared vocabulary, not something to over-weight.

**DISC** — measures behavioral/communication style (Dominance, Influence, Steadiness, Conscientiousness) rather than deep personality. Common in workplace and leadership-training contexts; could be a good later addition once Shiloh is doing real work-exposure (Doc 10) and wants language for how he works with others on a team.

## 7. Cross-System Themes (for Compass and Thomas to watch for, not conclusions to force)

A few threads show up more than once across genuinely independent systems, which makes them worth paying attention to without over-claiming certainty from any single one:

- **Lead / act / go first**, tempered by **precision and a wish to do it right** — Aries Sun + Mars in Virgo (Western), Leo Lagna (Vedic), Six Metal (Nine Star Ki), Manifesting Generator's "multi-hyphenate momentum" (Human Design) all point some version of this direction.
- **Freedom and honesty matter more to him emotionally than routine or certainty does** — Sagittarius Moon (Western) most directly, echoed by Human Design's "respond, don't force" strategy.
- **A private, deep, loyal layer under a more outwardly exploratory one** — the real tension between Sagittarius Moon (Western, outward-processing) and Scorpio Moon/Anuradha Nakshatra (Vedic, inward and slow-building) is worth naming directly rather than smoothing over; both can be true depending on context.
- **Real advancement may look later/slower than his peers, and that's fine, not a problem** — Anuradha Nakshatra's "advancement after 30" theme actually maps cleanly onto Doc 07's Seasons-of-Life table (the teen years are raw-material-building, not the season where the "real career" is supposed to already exist).
- **Original, not-a-follower, and built for leadership/influence roles** — Gene Keys' Life's Work (Gate 51, "not the kind of person to toe the line") and Pearl Sequence ("I thrive best in a position of leadership or influence") independently echo the Leo Lagna, Mars-in-Virgo, and Six Metal "natural anchor" themes above.
- **Communication as an unconscious gift, but real need for solitude to recharge it** — Gene Keys' Purpose (62) and Radiance (61) name this directly; worth reading alongside the Virgo Rising's quiet, observant first impression and the Sagittarius Moon's need for room to talk things out.
