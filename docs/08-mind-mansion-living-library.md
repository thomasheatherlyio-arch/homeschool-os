# Doc 08 — The Mind Mansion: Living Library

**Purpose:** a growing library of books, videos, and practices that furnish Shiloh's mind the way a mansion gets furnished room by room. Nothing here is finished on day one — the Mansion starts mostly empty, and it fills in as Shiloh actually reads, watches, and practices things. Every item lives in a specific room, and every room maps 1:1 to a quadrant of the Circle (Doc 01), so what goes into the Mansion visibly feeds the same wheel everything else in this system feeds.

---

## 1. The Concept

The Mansion is a memory-palace metaphor with a real job to do: instead of "books Shiloh should read" floating as a vague, guilt-inducing list, each one gets a *room* — a home, a reason it's there, and a visible link back to the Circle. Walking into a room and finding it empty is useful information, same as an empty quadrant slice at Friday review (Doc 04 §1) — it's a prompt to go furnish it, not a failure.

Like the Knights (Doc 02) and COLT (Doc 06), the Mansion is choose-your-own-adventure inside real structure: anyone (a Knight, COLT, Thomas, or Shiloh himself) can recommend something for a room, but it stays a *candidate* — sitting on the shelf, unconfirmed — until Shiloh actually picks it up. See Section 6.

The Mansion isn't only things recommended *to* Shiloh, either. Everything he actually finishes — every project, assignment, and video — moves in and lives here permanently too, in **The Gallery** (Section 8). The Mansion is where his mind gets furnished *and* where what he's built with it gets kept.

## 4. Turning a Book Into a Project — The Quest Menu

A reusable menu, first built for *Adventures of the Karu Mar* but meant to be reused for any book on the Study Shelves. The baseline is always a simple daily reading log (pages read, running total, one line/vocab word per day) — that alone is real Reading evidence per Doc 03 §4. On top of that baseline, Shiloh picks one finished-product option below (or stacks two) — deliberately spanning from "classic book report" all the way to "make a YouTube video," so the format is his choice, not a default.

| Option | Finished Product | Covers (Doc 03 §4) | Quadrant(s) (Doc 01) | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The Classic** | Written book report — plot, characters, conflict, honest take | Reading, Spelling & Grammar | 🧠 Mind & Mastery | Low |
| **Cartographer's Guild** | World atlas + bestiary of creatures/places encountered | Reading, Spelling & Grammar, light Science (classification) | 🧠 Mind & Mastery (+ 🚀 Venture & Craft if polished) | Medium |
| **Hero's Journey Map** | Map the protagonist's arc against classic story beats; what's being redeemed | Reading, Good Citizenship | 🧠 Mind & Mastery + 🕊️ Spirit & Character | Medium |
| **Book Review — NotebookLM Deck + Zoom Presentation** ⭐ *active for Karu Mar* | Word reading log → AI-generated slide deck → recorded Zoom presentation | Reading, Spelling & Grammar, Good Citizenship | 🧠 Mind & Mastery + 🚀 Venture & Craft | Medium-High |
| **Write the Sequel's Pitch** | Original outline or scene continuing the story | Reading, Spelling & Grammar, Systems & Self-Command | 🧠 Mind & Mastery + 🗂️ Systems & Self-Command | Medium-High |
| **Quest Night** | Runs a scavenger hunt/puzzle game built from a scene in the book | Reading, Systems & Self-Command | 🧠 Mind & Mastery + 🗂️ Systems & Self-Command (+ 🚀 Venture & Craft if run for others) | High |
| **In-Character Interview** | Recorded interview or debate in character / about a character's choices | Reading, Good Citizenship | 🧠 Mind & Mastery + 🕊️ Spirit & Character | Medium |

**Full pipeline for the Book Review option** — the five-step Word-log-to-deck-to-recording process, plus the fillable Word template — now lives in **Doc 11 §8, The Book Report Pipeline**. That's the concrete, reusable plan Shiloh is currently running for Karu Mar; every book after this one can reuse the same template and steps.

The **Book Review** and **In-Character Interview** options are also legitimate Doc 11 Studio pieces (Learning Log / Edutainment pillars, §2.1/§2.5) — a finished one can double-count as both a book project and a Studio episode. Finished products get logged in the Evidence Inbox (Doc 04 §2.3) and then filed permanently in The Gallery (Section 8); when the book itself is finished, its Study Shelves row moves to ✅ Finished with a one-line honest take from Shiloh.

## 8. The Gallery — Shiloh's Finished Work

Sections 3–6 are things *recommended to* Shiloh — books, videos, practices, tools waiting to be picked up. The Gallery is the opposite: it's every project, assignment, and video Shiloh actually **finishes**, kept here permanently once it's done. If the rest of the Mansion furnishes his mind, the Gallery is the trophy case showing what he's built with it — and, practically, it's Shiloh's own portfolio, the kind of thing worth pointing to later.

**What lands here:** anything completed, including —
- A finished Quest Menu book project (Section 4) — the report, the world atlas, the recorded presentation, whatever he built
- A finished Studio piece (Doc 11 §2) — a Learning Log clip, a mentor interview, a Day in the Life, hobby content, an Edutainment deep-dive
- A finished Book Report Pipeline recording + its source document (Doc 11 §8)
- A finished Minecraft Chronicles episode (Doc 11 §9)
- Any other completed project-level-contract deliverable (Doc 04 §3) or academic assignment worth keeping on the record

| Date Finished | Title | Type | Quadrant(s) | Related To | Where It Lives | Shiloh's One-Line Take |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| *— empty, ready to fill —* | | | | | | |

**How it fills:** the moment something's finished, it's captured same-day in the Evidence Inbox (Doc 04 §2.3) exactly as before — nothing changes about the daily capture habit. What's new is the next step: at Friday review, once a piece is reviewed and filed, it also gets a permanent row here, organized like the rest of the Mansion instead of staying in a flat weekly log. The Evidence Inbox is the in-tray; the Gallery is where things live after that.

**Why this matters beyond bragging rights:** Doc 05 §2 (Phase 2 Roadmap) names an "automated evidence portfolio" as a future goal — a clean, exportable record Thomas could show a school, program, or college later. The Gallery is the MVP, hand-kept version of exactly that, built the same conversational way as the rest of this system, starting now instead of waiting for Phase 2 automation.

## 9. How Items Get Added — Recommendation Protocol

1. **Anyone can propose.** A Knight mentor (in character), COLT, Thomas, or Shiloh can suggest a book, video, practice, or tool for a room, the same way Doc 02's open Knight seats work.
2. **It starts as a candidate.** A proposal goes on the shelf as 📖 / 🎬 Recommended, 🌱 Starting, or 💡 Candidate (for Toolshed items). It is not assigned or required — Shiloh confirms it by actually starting it, and Toolshed items additionally need Thomas's sign-off before setup (Section 7).
3. **Status moves forward honestly.** Recommended → Reading/Watching/Active → Finished, or → Shelved if it's paused. Shelved isn't a failure; it's the same "not done yet" honesty Doc 04's Friday review uses for missions.
4. **Finishing something worth logging gets a one-line reflection** from Shiloh — what he actually thought, not a book-report summary. That line is the evidence, and it's what goes into the Gallery entry (Section 8) once the piece is actually his own finished work rather than something recommended to him.

## 10. Connection to the Weekly Rhythm (Doc 04)

- A book, video, practice session, or tool finished/used during the week can be captured same-day in the **Evidence Inbox** (Doc 04 §2.3), the same as any other quadrant evidence.
- At Friday review, a finished item or an active tool's usage can factor into that room's quadrant score for the week — it's real, evidenced movement in that slice of the Circle.
- Also at Friday review, anything Shiloh actually *made* — a project, assignment, or video — that's now finished gets filed permanently into **The Gallery** (Section 8), separate from the consumption shelves.
- Big finishes (a completed book, a practice hitting a real streak, a banking account actually being used to save toward something, a Gallery piece Shiloh's genuinely proud of) are fair game to mention at the Quarterly Headmaster Review as part of the evidence portfolio.

## 12. Activation Protocol

To add, update, or browse the Mansion in conversation:

> "Let's add something to the Mansion" — or name the room directly: "Put this in the Workshop" / "Log a Chapel practice" / "Add a candidate to the Toolshed."

To do a quick furnishing check (mirrors the Circle snapshot in Doc 04):

> "Walk me through the Mansion — what's on the shelves, what's in progress, what rooms and tools are still just candidates?"

To build a project menu for a book already on the shelf:

> "Build me a Quest Menu for [book]" — reuses the format in Section 4. For the full production pipeline (Word log → NotebookLM deck → Zoom recording), see Doc 11 §8. For Minecraft Chronicles projects, see Doc 11 §9.

To file a finished piece:

> "Add this to the Gallery" — or just "Shiloh finished [project/video], log it" — walks through Section 8's table and, if it hasn't already, the Evidence Inbox (Doc 04 §2.3).

To pull up his portfolio:

> "Show me everything in the Gallery" / "What has Shiloh actually finished so far?"
