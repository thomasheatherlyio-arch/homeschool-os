# Doc 15 — The Gym: Personal Training & Fitness Science Course

**Purpose:** A structured, self-paced curriculum treating personal training as a legitimate career/life-skill track (Doc 10, Doc 13) and a vehicle for Texas-curriculum subject credit (Doc 03) — Health & PE most directly, but also Science, Math, Reading, and Good Citizenship — taught through a training log and a barbell. Runs alongside, not instead of, Shiloh's basketball work with Jahari (Doc 02); Jahari coaches the sport, this course teaches the underlying science of training.

## 1–3. Why This Fits / Quadrant Map / Texas Tie-In

Anchored in **Body & Discipline**, pulling hard into **Mind & Mastery**. Covers Health & PE (the whole course), Science (exercise physiology, kinesiology, the flat-feet case study), Mathematics (volume-load math, % of max, calorie/macro math), Reading (certification/research material), Spelling & Grammar (a written client program), Good Citizenship (coaching a real person responsibly, scope of practice), Executive Function (running a training log, planning/adjusting a real program).

## 4. Course Structure — 8 Units + Capstone

1. **Foundations & Safety** — anatomy, warm-up/cool-down, form, injury prevention.
2. **Exercise Physiology & Energy Systems** — ATP-PC/Glycolytic/Oxidative systems, muscle fiber types, aerobic vs. anaerobic.
3. **Types of Fitness — Training Disciplines** — powerlifting, bodybuilding, calisthenics, Olympic weightlifting, endurance, functional/mixed — compared by goal, method, energy system, fiber emphasis.
4. **Program Design & Progressive Overload** — sets/reps/volume, % of max, progression rules.
5. **Workout Tracking Systems** — volume load, RPE, logging methods.
6. **Nutrition Fundamentals** — macros, calorie needs, hydration, meal timing.
7. **Assessment & Corrective Exercise** — including Shiloh's own flat-feet biomechanics case study and a phased (Weeks 1-4, 3-8, ongoing) corrective protocol.
8. **The Business & Profession of Personal Training** — NASM/ACE/ISSA/ACSM/NSCA certification landscape, costs (~$900-1400), age-18 minimum, CPR/AED as an achievable-now credential.
**Capstone:** design and run a real program for an actual person (assess, program per the built template, coach one live session, log it, reflect).

## 5–19. Skill Placement, Deep Dives, Deliverables, Weekly Rhythm, Career Tie-In

Includes two built, reusable deliverables: a full **stretching/mobility routine** (dynamic warm-up + static cool-down, with flat-feet-specific moves folded in) and a **sample client workout plan** (4-day upper/lower split with a tracking log template). Career tie-in follows the same Doc 10 Rung 1→2 pattern (informational interview → gym shadow), with certification a genuine 4-year-out milestone worth studying now even though it can't be tested on yet. Mansion tie-in: furnishes Doc 08's previously-empty Training Hall room.

## 20. Deep Dive — Fasting Research (added 2026-08-24)

A cautious, well-sourced section concluding that **actual fasting protocols are not appropriate for Shiloh right now** (a 2025 TUM/Helmholtz Munich study found chronic intermittent fasting impaired pancreatic beta-cell maturation specifically in adolescent mice, and teen-athlete nutrition guidance runs the opposite direction — eat every 3-4 hours). Offers a safe alternative: Option A, a pure **observation log** of his already-existing overnight fasting window (no restriction, just data collection) as legitimate science-method practice instead.
