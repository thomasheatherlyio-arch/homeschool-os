# Doc 01 — The Circle: Holistic Growth Quadrants

**Purpose:** a single wheel that represents whole-life development, so academics never become the only thing that counts as "progress." The wheel is dynamic — quadrants can be added, merged, or renamed as Shiloh grows — but it needs a starting shape. Below is the v1.0 shape, built specifically around what we know about Shiloh right now.

---

## 1. The Five Slices (v1.0)

Most "wheel of life" templates default to four quadrants. This version uses **five**, because Shiloh's documented struggle wasn't a content gap — it was a systems gap — and that deserves its own slice rather than being buried inside "Academics." Thomas can collapse this back to four later if it stops being useful.

### 🧠 Mind & Mastery — Academics
Core content mastery: the five subjects Texas law requires (reading, spelling, grammar, mathematics, good citizenship), plus science, history/social studies, and any elective academic interest Shiloh develops. This is the quadrant Doc 03 tracks in detail for legal/compliance purposes.

### 💪 Body & Discipline — Health, Fitness, Sports
Physical training, nutrition, and sport — currently anchored by basketball. Chosen by Shiloh as one of his two strongest current interests. This is also where physical discipline and mental toughness get built, which is why it pairs naturally with Jahari's dual role (see Doc 02).

### 🚀 Venture & Craft — Entrepreneurship & Making
Building things, side hustles, flipping, content creation, anything that turns an idea into something real and (ideally) something that earns. Chosen by Shiloh as his second strongest current interest, and the natural home for the "experiential entrepreneurship" piece of this whole system — including the contract/agreement lessons from Doc 00.

### 🕊️ Spirit & Character — Spirituality, Ethics, Zen Practice
Values, character, self-reflection, and whatever spiritual or contemplative practice Shiloh wants to explore — currently anchored by Jahari's zen/spiritual mentorship alongside basketball. This quadrant also carries the "good citizenship" spirit of the Texas-required curriculum in its lived, not just textbook, form.

### 🗂️ Systems & Self-Command — Executive Function & Life Skills
Planning, time management, organization, follow-through, being prepared. This is the quadrant that exists specifically because of Shiloh's 7th-grade history: overwhelmed by too much at once, without a system to manage it. This is the meta-skill everything else depends on, and it gets tracked and coached directly instead of assumed.

---

## 2. How the Wheel Is Scored

Each quadrant gets a simple 0–10 fill score, updated at the Friday review (Doc 04). The score isn't a grade — it's a rough gut-check on "how much real, evidenced movement happened this week in this slice of life," discussed together by Shiloh and Thomas. A lopsided wheel (one or two slices maxed, others empty) is itself useful information and a normal, expected prompt to rebalance the next week's plan — not a failure.

## 3. Elective Expansion

As Shiloh's interests develop, new slices can be proposed (art & fashion, humanities & etiquette, and travel/culture were all raised as future candidates in early planning). Per Doc 00 Section 6, Shiloh can propose additions; Thomas approves. A slice should only be added if it's going to get real weekly attention — an unused seventh slice just dilutes the wheel.
