## 5. How to adjust

- Say **"reschedule"** to COLT to move a block, swap a course day, or shrink a sitting for one day or one week. COLT updates today's [[day-plan]] (and this note, if the standing template itself should change).
- The **daily check-in** uses this note: morning names today's blocks and the first physical action; wrap-up checks done / not done against the day's plan.
- One-off changes (a doctor's appointment, a Jahari session, a heavier Kitchen afternoon) do not rewrite the template. Standing changes need Shiloh and Dad to agree, then this draft is updated and — once times are approved — the Homie School calendar is mirrored again.
- Do not silently drop ELA or math to make room. Shift the clock instead. If the load is too heavy, follow [[khan-priority-plan]]: cut math keep-warm first, then one science block, not ELA, unless Dad says otherwise.
